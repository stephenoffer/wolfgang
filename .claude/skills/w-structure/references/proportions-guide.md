# Proportions and Pacing Guide

## Duration Calculation

### Formula
```
Duration (seconds) = (Bars x Beats_per_bar x 60) / Tempo_BPM
Duration (minutes) = Duration (seconds) / 60
```

### Theme and Variations (~200 bars total)

| Section | Bars | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Theme | 16-32 | Clear, simple, often binary or rounded binary |
| Each Variation | 16-32 | Usually same length as theme (or proportional: half, double) |
| Final Variation | 16-64 | Often extended, climactic, coda-like |
| Coda | 8-30 | Summation, may recall original theme |

**Variation pacing:** Start simple (ornamental), increase complexity, include one slow/modal change variation, build to virtuosic climax, end with return or apotheosis.

## Development Section Density

Development sections have higher harmonic, motivic, and rhythmic density compared to exposition.

| Parameter | Exposition | Development | Recapitulation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key changes / 8 bars | 0-1 | 2-4 | 0-1 |
| Motivic fragmentation | Low | High | Low-Medium |
| Sequential passages | Rare | Frequent | Rare |
| Rhythmic activity | Moderate | High | Moderate |
| Dynamic range per section | mp-f | pp-ff | mp-ff |
| Contrapuntal density | 2-3 voices | 3-5 voices | 2-4 voices |
| Phrase lengths | Regular (4+4) | Irregular (3+5, 2+2+4) | Regular |

## Transition Length Conventions

| Transition Type | Bars | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| Brief link | 4-8 | Scale passage, dominant pedal |
| Standard modulating | 12-24 | Sequential, building energy |
| Extended/dramatic | 24-40 | Multiple stages, climactic |
| Dissolution | 8-16 | Energy drains away, ritardando |
| Attacca | 0-4 | Direct connection between sections |

**Transition toolkit:**
1. Start with end of previous theme material
2. Fragment and sequence the motive
3. Accelerate harmonic rhythm
4. Arrive on dominant of new key (or just pivot)
5. Optional: dominant pedal for 2-8 bars before new theme

## Coda Proportions

| Context | Coda Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Classical (Haydn/Mozart) | 5-10% of movement | Brief, cadential, tonic confirmation |
| Beethoven | 10-20% of movement | Second development, new climax |
| Romantic | 10-15% of movement | Apotheosis of main theme |
| Concerto | 5-10% after cadenza | Orchestral summary, brilliant close |

## The Golden Section in Music

### Ratio: 0.618 (or 1.618 inverse)

**Application:** Place the main climax at 61.8% of the total duration.

| Total Duration | Climax At | Bar Count @ 132 BPM (4/4) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min (165 bars) | 3:05 (bar 102) | Bar 102 of 165 |
| 8 min (264 bars) | 4:57 (bar 163) | Bar 163 of 264 |
| 10 min (330 bars) | 6:11 (bar 204) | Bar 204 of 330 |
| 12 min (396 bars) | 7:25 (bar 245) | Bar 245 of 396 |
| 15 min (495 bars) | 9:16 (bar 306) | Bar 306 of 495 |

**Nested golden sections:** Apply within sections too:
- Exposition climax at 61.8% of exposition
- Development climax at 61.8% of development
- Each results in a fractal-like tension structure

### Other Significant Proportions
| Ratio | Placement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0.500 | Midpoint | Symmetric, classical balance |
| 0.618 | Golden section | Natural, satisfying tension arc |
| 0.750 | Three-quarters | Late climax, extended resolution |
| 0.333 / 0.667 | Thirds | Good for ABA structures |
| 0.800+ | Very late climax | Maximizes tension, brief resolution |

## Pacing Principles

### Tension Accumulation Techniques
| Technique | Implementation | Bars Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Harmonic acceleration | Chords change every 2 bars -> every bar -> every beat | 8-24 |
| Rhythmic intensification | Half notes -> quarters -> eighths -> sixteenths | 8-16 |
| Registral expansion | Lines spread outward from middle | 4-16 |
| Dynamic crescendo | pp through to ff | 8-32 |
| Textural buildup | Solo -> chamber -> tutti | 16-48 |
| Melodic ascending sequence | Sequence steps upward | 4-12 |
| Pedal point | Sustained bass, increasing dissonance above | 8-24 |
| Stretto | Entries increasingly closer together | 4-16 |

### Release / Resolution Techniques
| Technique | Implementation | Bars Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Sudden drop | Tutti ff to solo pp | 1-2 |
| Gradual diminuendo | ff through to pp | 8-24 |
| Rhythmic deceleration | Fast notes slow to sustained | 4-12 |
| Registral contraction | Wide spacing narrows to middle | 4-8 |
| Harmonic resolution | Dissonance resolves to consonance | 1-4 |
| Textural thinning | Instruments drop out | 8-16 |
| Codetta figures | Repeated cadential patterns | 4-12 |

### Climax Placement Rules
1. **Single climax:** Place at golden section (0.618) for maximum effect
2. **Double climax:** First at ~0.400, second (larger) at ~0.800
3. **Progressive climaxes:** Each wave peaks higher: 0.25, 0.50, 0.75 (largest)
4. **Anti-climax:** Expected climax undercut — powerful for tragedy/irony

### False Climaxes
Build toward a climax but deflect before reaching it. Resume building from a lower energy level. Creates expectation and eventual greater satisfaction when the true climax arrives. Allow 8-16 bars of relative calm between false and true climax.

## Rest and Breathing Space

### Between Sections
| Transition Type | Silence/Rest | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Grand pause (G.P.) | 1-2 beats of silence | Before dramatic return, after climax |
| Fermata | Sustained final note | End of section, before attacca |
| Ritardando + rest | Slowing + brief rest | Between exposition and development |
| Attacca | No break | Between closely related sections |
| Breath mark | Brief lift | Between phrases, soloistic moments |

### Pacing Rests in Fast Movements
- 2-4 bars of reduced texture every 30-50 bars
- At least one quiet passage between every two forte passages
- Brief unison passages offer "reset" moments for the ear

### Pacing in Slow Movements
- Longer phrases (8-12 bars) before cadence
- Allow solo instruments to breathe naturally (rests in melody)
- Avoid continuous thick texture; alternate full scoring with chamber moments
- Space between climactic moments: at least 16 bars of calm

## Tempo Relationships Between Movements

### Common Relationships
| Mvt I -> Mvt II | Relationship | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Allegro q=132 -> Adagio q=66 | Half tempo | Exact 2:1 proportion |
| Allegro q=132 -> Andante q=88 | 2:3 ratio | Smooth transition |
| Allegro q=144 -> Largo q=48 | 3:1 ratio | Strong contrast |

### Cross-Movement Tempo Proportions
| Movement Pair | Common Ratio | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| I fast : II slow | 2:1 or 3:1 | Standard contrast |
| II slow : III scherzo | 1:2 or 1:3 | Energy increase |
| III scherzo : IV finale | ~1:1 or 3:4 | Maintained or increased energy |
| I fast : IV finale | 3:4 or 1:1 | Finale slightly faster or equal |

## Summary: Planning a Movement

1. **Choose form** (sonata, rondo, ternary, etc.)
2. **Set total duration** target in minutes
3. **Set tempo** -> calculate total bars
4. **Divide bars** into sections using proportion tables above
5. **Place climax** at golden section (~0.618)
6. **Plan density curve:** light -> medium -> heavy -> climax -> resolution
7. **Check transitions:** allocate 5-10% of total bars to transitions
8. **Plan breathing:** ensure at least 2-4 bars of reduced texture every 30-50 bars
9. **Verify duration** with formula: (bars x beats x 60) / BPM
