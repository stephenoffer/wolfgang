# Igor Stravinsky — Index

**Era:** Modern | **Nationality:** Russian (later French/American) | **1882–1971**

**Key Works (by period):**
- Russian: Firebird, Petrushka, The Rite of Spring, Pulcinella, The Soldier's Tale
- Neo-Classical: Symphony in C, Octet, Symphony of Psalms, Orpheus, The Rake's Progress
- Serial: Agon, Threni, Requiem Canticles, Movements for Piano and Orchestra

**Style Summary (THREE COMPLETELY DIFFERENT AESTHETICS — specify period):**
- **Russian (1910–1920):** Block juxtaposition (hard cuts between musical blocks), ostinato hypnosis, polytonality (Petrushka chord: C major + F# major simultaneously), asymmetric changing meter, pagan roughness.
- **Neo-Classical (1920–1951):** Tonal but deliberately "wrong" harmonizations, Baroque formal containers (fugue, passacaglia, ritornello) with modern rhythmic language, dry non-vibrato orchestral sound, ironic quotation.
- **Serial (1953–1971):** Twelve-tone rows, austere and ritualistic rather than expressionistic, sparse textures, long silences, chant-like vocal writing.
