# Dmitri Shostakovich — Index

**Era:** Modern | **Nationality:** Russian/Soviet | **1906–1975**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 5, 7 (Leningrad), 8, 10, 13, 14, 15; String Quartets 1–15 (esp. 8); Piano Quintet; Piano Concertos 1–2; String Quartet No.8; Preludes and Fugues Op.87; Lady Macbeth of the Mtsensk District (opera); The Nose (opera)

**Style Summary:**
- Composer under Soviet oppression: Shostakovich survived Stalin, Nazi invasion, and ideological condemnation. Every compositional choice carries the weight of survival and coded resistance.
- Ironic major-key grotesquerie: bright, cheerful passages (staccato, march tempo, ff) that mean the OPPOSITE of what they sound. The forced cheerfulness IS the horror.
- Obsessive ostinato as oppression: mechanical repeated bass patterns continue relentlessly while the upper voices express increasing distress. The relentlessness IS the statement.
- DSCH motif: D-Eb-C-B (German notation D-Es-C-H) — his musical signature, embedded in many themes.
- Extreme register at climaxes: piccolo at its highest, contrabass at its lowest. The physical discomfort IS the compositional intent.
