# Olivier Messiaen — Index

**Era:** Modern | **Nationality:** French | **1908–1992**

**Key Works:** Quartet for the End of Time, Turangalîla-Symphonie, Catalogue d'oiseaux (piano), Vingt regards sur l'Enfant-Jésus (piano), La Nativité du Seigneur (organ), Méditations sur le mystère de la Sainte Trinité (organ), Des canyons aux étoiles, Saint François d'Assise (opera)

**Style Summary:**
- Catholic mystic: Messiaen's music is explicitly theological. The Quartet for the End of Time comes from the Book of Revelation. Transcendence is a compositional goal.
- Modes of limited transposition: self-invented scales (Mode 2 = octatonic, Mode 3, etc.) that cannot be fully transposed — they repeat after 2–5 transpositions. Each mode has a specific character: Mode 2 = supernatural; Mode 3 = prismatic luminosity.
- Bird song: Messiaen transcribed hundreds of bird songs. The Catalogue d'oiseaux (13 pieces) depicts specific birds in specific landscapes. Bird calls appear as rapid, high, irregular melodic figures throughout his work.
- Color chords: Messiaen was synesthetic — he experienced harmonies as colors. His "color chords" (stacked 9ths, 11ths, 13ths in specific voicings) are designed to produce specific visual-color experiences.
- Rhythmic innovation: non-retrogradable rhythms (palindromes), added values, Greek/Hindu rhythmic modes.
