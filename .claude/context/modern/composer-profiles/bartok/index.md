# Béla Bartók — Index

**Era:** Modern | **Nationality:** Hungarian | **1881–1945**

**Key Works:** String Quartets 1–6, Music for Strings Percussion and Celesta, Concerto for Orchestra, Piano Concerto No.3, Violin Concerto No.2, Mikrokosmos (153 piano pieces), Bluebeard's Castle (opera), Cantata Profana, Dance Suite

**Style Summary:**
- Ethnomusicologist and composer: Bartók collected over 10,000 folk songs from Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Turkey, and North Africa. This field research IS his musical DNA — not decoration but source material.
- Folk-derived melodic cells: pentatonic patterns, specific folk scales (Lydian with raised 4th, Phrygian with lowered 2nd), asymmetric phrase lengths (5 or 7 bars, not 4 or 8).
- Axis system tonality: tritone-related keys function as equivalents. C and F# are interchangeable tonal centers. Movement C→F# feels like arriving at the tonic.
- Night music texture: mysterious, static passages — chromatic clusters, flutter-tongue flutes, string harmonics, irregular ppp pizzicato. Nocturnal atmosphere without pulse.
- Bulgarian/Balkan asymmetric rhythms: 5/8 (2+3 or 3+2), 7/8 (2+2+3), 8/8 (3+2+3). Felt as patterns of long and short, not counted as beats.
