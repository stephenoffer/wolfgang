# Bartók Left-Hand Vocabulary

**A caveat first.** Bartók's armed corpus is **16 bars** — one short piece — so
no habit can be measured from it and `voicing_profile` refuses to report. Those
bars are in **2/4**, left hand entirely `block_chord_sparse`, harmony `i` /
`III` / `iv`, which is consistent with the folk-derived language below but
proves nothing. Everything here is craft knowledge.

The organising fact: **the piano is a percussion instrument.** Bartók said so
explicitly. The left hand strikes, and its dissonances are struck deliberately,
not resolved.

## The patterns

1. **Struck dissonant blocks** — a chord containing a second or a seventh,
   played hard and not resolved. The seconds are the sound.
   `[C3,D3,G3]e [C3,D3,G3]e`

2. **Ostinato in an irregular metre** — a short figure repeated exactly under a
   changing metre, the Bulgarian rhythms of 5/8, 7/8 and 9/8 grouped 2+2+3.
   `C2e G2e C2e G2e C2e`

3. **Bare open fifths** — the peasant bagpipe drone, held or repeated, under a
   modal tune.
   `[D2,A2]w`

4. **Parallel motion in fourths or fifths** — both hands moving in rigid
   parallel, deliberately breaking the part-writing rules.

5. **Mirror inversion** — the left hand playing the right hand's line upside
   down, note for note. A structural device in the Mikrokosmos and the concerti.

6. **Cluster** — adjacent notes struck together with the flat of the hand or as
   a written chord, for percussive attack.

7. **Night-music stillness** — isolated low notes and dissonant specks against
   silence, with no pulse. His most personal texture, and the opposite of
   everything above.

## Rhythm

Metre changes bar to bar. Accents fall against the barline. Grouping in 2+2+3
rather than 3+4 is the essential Bulgarian inflection; write the accents in.

## What to avoid

- **Resolving the dissonances.** A second is a colour, not a suspension.
- **A steady four-square metre.** If the metre never changes, it is not Bartók.
- **Romantic rubato.** The rhythm is motoric and exact.
- **Functional tonic-dominant harmony.** The centre is asserted by insistence,
  not by cadence.
- **Pedal blur.** The attack must stay percussive and dry.
