# Sergei Prokofiev — Orchestration

Prokofiev uses a Classical orchestra in a 20th-century way. The forces are often modest — closer to Haydn than to Strauss — but the writing is percussive, brilliant, and athletic. Instruments are treated as soloists, each with a specific character. The piano holds a special place: it is Prokofiev's primary instrument and appears in his orchestral music as a percussive, concertante voice. His ballet scoring is among the finest in the repertoire — narrative, colorful, and dramatically precise.

## Core Orchestral Character

| Feature | Fast Movements | Slow Movements | Ballet/Character |
|---------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Texture | Lean, transparent; Classical clarity | Warm strings + solo wind | Narrative coloring; each character = timbre |
| Strings | Percussive; staccato; driving rhythm | Cantabile; divided for warmth | Lyrical or grotesque depending on character |
| Winds | Brilliant solos; agile runs | Sustained; oboe or clarinet solo | Character-defining: flute = bird, clarinet = cat |
| Brass | Rhythmic punctuation; fanfares | Minimal; horn sustains | Heroic or threatening depending on context |
| Percussion | Active; drives momentum | Minimal or absent | Narrative punctuation |
| Piano | Concertante; percussive chords | Absent or accompaniment | Orchestral color; dry, brilliant |

## Typical Orchestra

| Work Type | Forces | Character |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Symphony | 2.2.2.2 / 4.2.3.1 / timp+perc / strings | Classical-sized; lean and athletic |
| Piano concerto | Solo piano + 2.2.2.2 / 4.2.3.1 / timp+perc / strings | Piano as percussive protagonist |
| Ballet (large) | 3.3.3.3 / 4.3.3.1 / timp+3perc / harp / strings | Full color palette; narrative demands |
| Suite | 2.2.2.2 / 4.2.3.0 / timp+perc / strings | Chamber-scale orchestral |
| *Peter and the Wolf* | Narrator + fl, ob, cl, bsn, 3 hn, tpt, tbn, timp, perc, strings | Each instrument = character |

## Instrument Roles — Detailed

### Strings

| Technique | Usage | Character |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Staccato repeated chords | Fast movements; accompaniment | Motoric drive; percussive |
| Cantabile melody (vln 1) | Slow themes; ballet love scenes | Genuine warmth; the singing Prokofiev |
| Pizzicato | March accompaniment; grotesque | Dry, witty, sharp |
| Tremolo, sul ponticello | Dramatic tension; battle scenes | Menacing, nervous |
| Divisi violas/cellos | Lyrical passages; warm inner voices | Rich middle-register color |
| Bass ostinato | Motoric passages; toccata style | Relentless machine; drives tempo |

### Woodwinds

| Instrument | Prokofiev's Signature Use | Key Moments |
|-----------|--------------------------|-------------|
| Flute | Bird-like agility; high brilliant runs; Peter's bird | *Peter and the Wolf*, Classical Symphony |
| Oboe | Nasal, plaintive solo; duck character | *Peter and the Wolf*, Romeo slow mvts |
| Clarinet | Agile, dark, sly character; cat | *Peter and the Wolf*, Piano Concerto No. 3 |
| Bassoon | Grandfather character; low comic; grumbling | *Peter and the Wolf*, Symphony No. 5 scherzo |
| Piccolo | Shrill climax; grotesque high point | Scythian Suite, Symphony No. 5 |

### Brass

| Instrument | Usage | Character |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Horn | Sustained pedals; hunting calls; heroic | Wolf theme (*Peter*); Symphony No. 5 |
| Trumpet | Fanfare; rhythmic attacks; military | *Alexander Nevsky*; march themes |
| Trombone | Power at climaxes; chorale-like passages | Battle music; *Alexander Nevsky* |
| Tuba | Bass weight; rare solo use | Foundation in ff tutti |

### Percussion

| Instrument | Usage | Character |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Timpani | Rhythmic drive; thematic (tuned to key pitches) | All symphonies; bass support |
| Snare drum | March rhythm; military character | *Lieutenant Kije*, marches |
| Bass drum | Climactic weight; battle scenes | *Alexander Nevsky* |
| Xylophone | Grotesque brightness; skeletal | *Romeo and Juliet* (Tybalt's death) |
| Tambourine | Dance character; folk | Ballet dance numbers |
| Castanets | Spanish character; dance | *Romeo and Juliet* |

## Peter and the Wolf — Orchestration as Character

| Character | Instrument | Register | Articulation | Key Trait |
|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Peter | Strings (full section) | Middle | Legato, confident | The hero: warm, clear, bright |
| Bird | Flute | High | Quick, fluttering, staccato | Agile, nervous, chatty |
| Duck | Oboe | Middle | Legato, waddling rhythm | Nasal, plaintive, slightly comic |
| Cat | Clarinet | Low-middle | Staccato, sly, chromatic | Sneaky, dark, smooth |
| Grandfather | Bassoon | Low | Grumbling, legato | Stern, comic, heavy |
| Wolf | 3 Horns | Low-middle | Sustained, menacing | Dark, powerful, threatening |
| Hunters | Timpani + bass drum | Low | March rhythm | Military, heavy |

## Texture Types

| Texture | Description | Where Used |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Melody + motoric bass | Solo melody over mechanical repeated-note bass | Fast sonata movements |
| Antiphonal dialogue | Two instruments or groups alternate | Ballet: character conversations |
| Tutti unison | Full orchestra on same melody, octave doublings | Climactic moments; Symphony No. 5 |
| Chamber reduction | 2-3 solo instruments exposed | Slow movements; intimate scenes |
| Percussive tutti | Full orchestra playing staccato, rhythmic chords | Motoric passages; battle music |
| Solo + sustained strings | Wind solo over held string chords | Lyrical second themes |

## Dynamic Approach

| Dynamic | Prokofiev Character | Typical Scoring |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| pp | Fairy-tale quiet; mysterious | Solo instrument + pizzicato |
| p | Lyrical warmth; cantabile | Strings + solo wind |
| mf | Active, clear, athletic | Winds + light strings |
| f | Energetic, brilliant; the default Prokofiev | Full ensemble; staccato |
| ff | Percussive power; climax | Tutti; brass + timpani |
| sfz | The "stab" — sudden accent on weak beat | Wrong-note harmony highlighted |
