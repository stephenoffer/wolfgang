# Steve Reich — Biography

|------|-------|----------------|

## Formative Influences

| Influence | What Reich Absorbed | How It Appears |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| Tape technology | Identical loops drift apart → phasing | *It's Gonna Rain*, *Come Out* |
| Ghanaian Ewe drumming | Interlocking patterns, polyrhythm, master drummer tradition | *Drumming*, all ensemble works |
| Balinese gamelan | Metallic timbres, interlocking kotekan patterns | Mallet instrument writing |
| John Coltrane / jazz | Modal harmony, rhythmic energy, ensemble improvisation | Pulse-based ensemble texture |
| Wittgenstein (philosophy) | "Don't think, look" — process over intention | Process as content; perceptible structure |
| Cantillation (Hebrew) | Speech melody, Torah chanting patterns | *Tehillim*, *The Cave*, *Daniel Variations* |

## The Phasing Discovery (1965)

| What Happened | Why It Mattered |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Two identical tape loops of a preacher ("It's gonna rain") ran on separate machines | The machines ran at slightly different speeds |
| The loops gradually drifted out of sync | New rhythmic patterns emerged from the interaction |
| Reich realized this was a compositional technique | Phasing: two identical patterns gradually shifting relationship |
| Live performance version: one player gradually speeds up | *Piano Phase* (1967): first live phasing piece |
