# Philip Glass — Index

**Era:** Minimalist | **Nationality:** American | **born 1937**

**Key Works:** Einstein on the Beach (opera), Satyagraha (opera), Akhnaten (opera), Glassworks, Metamorphosis (piano), Low Symphony, Heroes Symphony, Violin Concerto, String Quartets 2–5, Koyaanisqatsi (film score), The Hours (film score)

**Style Summary:**
- The accessible minimalist: Glass uses repetition and process like Reich, but with more harmonic warmth and a more approachable melodic surface.
- Additive process: a short pattern (3 eighth notes) is repeated, then one note is added (4 eighth notes), repeated, then another (5 notes). The process continues until a longer pattern is reached, then reverses subtractively.
- Arpeggiated keyboard figuration: his most characteristic texture — rapid, continuous arpeggiated patterns cycling through chord tones. The chord is embedded in the arpeggio.
- Modal harmony cycling: 3–4 chords from a modal scale (Dorian is characteristic), each lasting 4–8 bars, cycling without goal or resolution. The sequence repeats 6–8 times.
- Slow harmonic rhythm under fast surface: the arpeggio moves at 8th-note speed; the harmony changes every 4–8 bars. This contrast creates the Glass hypnotic effect.
