# Arvo Pärt — Index

**Era:** Minimalist / Sacred | **Nationality:** Estonian | **born 1935**

**Key Works:** Spiegel im Spiegel, Für Alina, Fratres, Tabula Rasa, Cantus in Memoriam Benjamin Britten, Passio, Berliner Messe, Te Deum, Miserere, Symphony No.3 (pre-tintinnabuli), Tintinnabuli works (post-1976)

**Style Summary:**
- Inventor of tintinnabuli (1976): after years of silence and study of Gregorian chant and early polyphony, Pärt invented his own technique. Two simultaneous voices: T-voice (arpeggiates only the tonic triad) and M-voice (moves stepwise in the diatonic scale). The relationship between them IS the music.
- Stepwise diatonic movement only: the M-voice never leaps. All melody is stepwise. The entire piece is generated by where the voice starts and how it moves.
- Silence as structural material equal to notes: a bar of silence between phrases is a phrase. The silences are not rests — they are the music breathing.
- Single tonal center, no modulation: one key, one triad, throughout. No development. No harmonic tension-release. The piece deepens within one space.
- Spiritual practice as compositional method: the simplicity IS the practice. Complexity would destroy it.
