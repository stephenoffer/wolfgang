# Jean Sibelius — Index

**Era:** Nationalistic | **Nationality:** Finnish | **1865–1957**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 1–7, Violin Concerto, Finlandia, En Saga, The Swan of Tuonela, Tapiola, Voiless (late), Kullervo Symphony, String Quartet in D minor

**Style Summary (Early vs. Late — DO NOT CONFLATE):**
- **Early Sibelius (Finlandia, Violin Concerto, Symphonies 1–2):** Strong nationalistic melody, clear Romantic orchestral rhetoric, dramatic climaxes, folk-like character, patriotic/heroic subject matter.
- **Late Sibelius (Symphonies 6–7, Tapiola, Voiless):** The definitive voice. Frozen pedal stasis (sustained bass notes while harmonies appear above), motivic cells that accumulate rather than develop, Nordic modal language (no leading tone, no dominant), orchestral texture as landscape, structural organicism (the form grows like a living thing).

**Silence (1927–1957):** Sibelius stopped composing after Tapiola and lived another 30 years in silence. The Eighth Symphony was destroyed or never completed.
