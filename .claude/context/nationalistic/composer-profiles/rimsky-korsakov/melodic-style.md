# Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov — Melodic Style

Rimsky-Korsakov's melody is a storyteller's voice. Where Mussorgsky's melody speaks, Rimsky's melody spins tales — decorated, ornamental, exotic. The Scheherazade violin solo is the paradigm: a long, sinuous line with trills at peaks, grace notes at phrase beginnings, and the personality of the specific instrument woven into every note. His melodies are never abstract — they are always a specific character, in a specific place, telling a specific story.

## Core Melodic Character

| Feature | Description | Where It Appears |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Decorated solo lines | Melodies written for specific instruments, with ornaments that exploit the instrument's character | Scheherazade violin, Capriccio espagnol clarinet, Sadko gusli imitation |
| Exotic modal inflection | Augmented 2nds, Phrygian lowered 2nd, harmonic minor 7th — specific interval colors | Scheherazade, Antar, all oriental subjects |
| Russian folk contour | Arched shape; pentatonic skeleton; modal; stepwise with occasional 4th/5th leaps | Folk scenes in operas, Russian Easter Overture |
| Coloratura fairy-tale vocal lines | Soprano roles for supernatural characters: high, decorated, florid, otherworldly | Snow Maiden, Volkhova (Sadko), Queen of Shemakha (Golden Cockerel) |
| Octatonic melody | Melodies using alternating whole-half steps for magic characters | Snow Maiden's supernatural music, Kashchei |
| Narrative instrumental melody | The melody tells a story; sequential phrases build narrative tension | Scheherazade's four movements |

## The Ornamental Melodic Line

Rimsky's solos are not bare melodies with optional ornaments — the ornaments ARE the melody. Remove the trills, grace notes, and turns, and the melodic character collapses.

| Ornament | Function | Placement |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Trill | Emphasis, brilliance, climactic intensification | At phrase peaks; on the highest note |
| Grace note (upper) | Anticipation, lightness, storytelling gesture | Phrase beginnings; approaching a main note from above |
| Grace note (lower) | Yearning, leaning into the next note | Before stepwise ascent |
| Turn | Decorative elaboration; spins around the main note | Mid-phrase; on sustained notes |
| Gruppetto (extended turn) | Elaborate decoration; the melody luxuriates on one pitch | Cadential passages; Scheherazade-type arabesques |
| Rapid scale passage | Virtuosic display; the instrument shows its personality | Between phrases; connecting sections |

```abc
X:1
T:Rimsky — Scheherazade Solo (decorated violin melody)
M:4/4
L:1/16
K:Am
%% Every ornament is integral; remove them and the melody loses its identity
!p!{B}A2Bc d2cB A2GF E2FG|{c}A4 z2AB c2Bc d2ef|
{e}d4 c2Bc A4 z4|
%% Grace notes, rapid runs, wide range — the storyteller's instrument
```

## Exotic Melody — The Augmented 2nd as Signature

The augmented 2nd (F-G# in A minor) is Rimsky's exotic color marker. It appears in:

| Context | Scale Source | Effect |
|---------|------------|--------|
| Arabian/Persian | Harmonic minor (A-B-C-D-E-F-G#) | Exotic tension; the interval IS the Orient |
| Spanish | Phrygian dominant (E-F-G#-A-B-C-D) | Flamenco brilliance; Spanish fire |
| Hindu | Double harmonic (A-Bb-C#-D-E-F-G#) | Maximum exoticism; two augmented 2nds |
| Russian supernatural | Octatonic (various) | Otherworldly; neither major nor minor |

```abc
X:2
T:Rimsky — Exotic Melody with Augmented 2nd (Scheherazade character)
M:3/4
L:1/8
K:Am
%% The augmented 2nd F-G# is approached and left by step
!p!A2 B2 c2|d2 c2 B2|A2 F2 ^G2|A6|
%% Step up to the F, then the augmented 2nd leap to G# — the exotic moment
```

```abc
X:3
T:Rimsky — Double Harmonic Scale Melody (maximum exoticism)
M:4/4
L:1/8
K:Am
%% Double harmonic: A-Bb-C#-D-E-F-G#-A — two augmented 2nds
A2 _B2 ^C2 D2|E2 F2 ^G2 A2|^G2 F2 E2 D2|^C2 _B2 A4|
%% Bb-C# and F-G#: both augmented 2nds active in one melody
```

## Russian Folk Melody

| Contour Type | Shape | Character | Where Used |
|-------------|-------|-----------|------------|
| Arched (protyazhnaya) | Rise-peak-descend; stepwise | Long-drawn-out Russian folk song | Slow themes; Russian Easter |
| Dance (plyas) | Narrow range, repetitive, rhythmic drive | Fast folk dance | May Night, Snow Maiden folk scenes |
| Lullaby/lament | Narrow range, descending, minor | Gentle or sorrowful | Slow opera scenes |
| Heroic (bylina) | Declamatory; repeated notes; 4th/5th leaps | Epic narrative; bard style | Sadko's gusli songs |

```abc
X:4
T:Rimsky — Russian Folk Melody (Russian Easter character)
M:4/4
L:1/8
K:Dm
%% Russian liturgical folk: arched, stepwise, modal
!mf!D2 E2 F2 G2|A2 G2 F2 E2|D2 C2 D4|
%% Dorian mode; arched contour; no augmented 2nd — this is the "real" Russian world
```

## Fairy-Tale Vocal Lines

Rimsky's supernatural female characters sing differently from his human characters — higher, more decorated, more virtuosic.

| Character Type | Vocal Character | Register | Ornaments |
|---------------|----------------|----------|-----------|
| Supernatural soprano (Snow Maiden, Volkhova) | Florid, high, decorated; coloratura | High soprano (C5-C6) | Trills, rapid scales, wide leaps |
| Human soprano (Marfa, Militrisa) | Lyrical, warm, less decorated | Middle soprano (A4-A5) | Simpler ornaments; folk-song contour |
| Mezzo/contralto (Lel, Spring) | Warm, pastoral, folk-like | Lower (G4-G5) | Minimal ornament; pure melody |
| Bass (kings, priests) | Declamatory, weight, authority | Low (C3-E4) | No ornament; block phrasing |

## Melodic Intervals

| Interval | Frequency | Context |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Step (2nd) | Very high | Stepwise motion in folk and decorated lines |
| Augmented 2nd | Signature | Exotic/oriental melodies — THE Rimsky interval |
| Minor 3rd | Common | Folk pentatonic; also in octatonic melody |
| Perfect 4th | Moderate | Folk song openings; heroic gestures; bylina |
| Perfect 5th | Moderate | Open, pastoral; drone relationship |
| Octave | For emphasis | Dramatic leaps in climactic passages |
| Tritone | In octatonic | Magic/supernatural melodies; unstable |

## Guide for Composing Rimsky-Style Melody

| Principle | Realization |
|-----------|-------------|
| Decorate the solo | Every solo needs trills at peaks, grace notes at entrances; bare melody is not Rimsky |
| Match ornament to instrument | Violin: rapid passages, double stops. Clarinet: chalumeau warmth. Flute: high trills |
| Augmented 2nd for exotic | Include at least one augmented 2nd in any oriental/exotic melody |
| Octatonic for magic | Supernatural melodies use alternating W-H steps |
| Wide range for supernatural voices | Fairy-tale soprano: 2 octaves. Human voice: 1.5 octaves |
| Narrative arc | Melody should build across phrases — not just repeat; the story progresses |
