# Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov — Index

**Era:** Nationalistic | **Nationality:** Russian | **1844–1908**

**Key Works:** Scheherazade, Spanish Capriccio, The Flight of the Bumblebee, Sadko (opera), The Snow Maiden (opera), The Golden Cockerel (opera), Russian Easter Overture, Principles of Orchestration (textbook)

**Style Summary:**
- The orchestration master: Rimsky-Korsakov's Principles of Orchestration remains the definitive manual of Late Romantic orchestration. He thinks in colors — each instrument is a specific color.
- Octatonic scale for the supernatural: alternating whole-half steps (C-D-Eb-F-F#-G#-A-B) used for fantasy, magic, and demonic characters throughout his operas.
- Arabian Nights exotic modal color: harmonic minor (augmented 2nd between 6th and 7th degrees), Arabic double-harmonic scale, Phrygian mode — for Scheherazade and other Eastern subjects.
- Brilliant solo woodwind writing: each solo crafted for the specific personality of the instrument. The Scheherazade violin solo, the bassoon in Peter and the Wolf (same tradition), the clarinet in the Spanish Capriccio.
- Visual, narrative orchestration: the sea (Scheherazade), the sunrise, the bumblebee — specific images created through specific orchestral techniques (string tremolo = shimmer, flutter-tongue = insect wings).
