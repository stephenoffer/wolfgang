# Grieg Left-Hand Vocabulary & Phrase Breathing

Grieg writes **miniatures with a strong modal-folk colour**. The left hand is
often sparse and always characterful: drones, open fifths, and dance rhythms drawn
from Norwegian practice.

## LH idiom catalog
1. **Drone fifths** — `[D2,A2]w` held under a modal melody. The Hardanger fiddle
   sound and his most characteristic device.
2. **Open fifths moving in parallel** — `[D2,A2]h [E2,B2]h`, bare and archaic.
3. **Springar / halling dance rhythm** — a lilting 3/4 with an uneven second beat.
4. **Chromatic inner voice over a static bass** — his standard intensifier in the
   lyric pieces.
5. **Arpeggiated wide chords**, warm, in the Romantic manner.
6. **Repeated chords, quiet**, under a simple melody.
7. **Bare octaves** for a stark or heroic statement.
8. **Sustained chord with a moving top voice** — the accompaniment carries a
   counter-melody.
9. **Bass in octaves for weight** at a climax of a short piece.
10. **Silence** — the miniatures use rests generously.

## Where the idiom changes
Between the sections of a ternary lyric piece, and between dance types.

## Breathing
Very clear four- and eight-bar phrases; the pieces are short and every phrase
cadences.

## What to avoid
- **Large-scale development.** The miniature is the form.
- **Dense counterpoint.**
- **Losing the modal colour.** Straight functional harmony sounds generic.
