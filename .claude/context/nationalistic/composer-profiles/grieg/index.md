# Edvard Grieg — Index

**Era:** Nationalistic | **Nationality:** Norwegian | **1843–1907**

**Key Works:** Piano Concerto in A minor, Peer Gynt Suites 1–2, Lyric Pieces (10 books, 66 pieces), Holberg Suite, String Quartet in G minor, Violin Sonatas 1–3, Songs (op.15, 18, 26, 33, 48, 60, 67, 69, 70)

**Style Summary:**
- The atmospheric miniaturist: Grieg's natural habitat is the short character piece (1–4 minutes). The Lyric Pieces are his most personal works. Each piece captures one atmosphere completely.
- Norwegian raised 4th (Lydian inflection): D# in A minor context — the Hardanger fiddle characteristic note. Brief, ornamental, immediately recognizable.
- Open-5th drone bass imitating Hardanger fiddle: A2–E3 sustained while harmonies above shift freely.
- Sudden tonal juxtaposition: A minor → F# major (major 3rd away) without preparation. A different light, not a modulation.
- Pedal point under changing harmonies: the rootedness of the Norwegian landscape with shifting harmonic "weather" above.
