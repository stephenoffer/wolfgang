# Modest Mussorgsky — Index

**Era:** Nationalistic | **Nationality:** Russian | **1839–1881**

**Key Works:** Pictures at an Exhibition (piano, also Ravel orchestration), Boris Godunov (opera), Khovanshchina (opera, unfinished), Night on Bald Mountain, Songs and Dances of Death, The Nursery (song cycle), Without Sun (song cycle)

**Style Summary:**
- The unpolished Russian: Mussorgsky rejected academic European rules deliberately. His harmony has parallel 5ths, unresolved dissonances, and abrupt chord connections — intentional, not ignorant.
- Speech-melody: vocal writing follows natural Russian speech rhythm (syllable stress determines note length), not Italian opera convention. Every line sounds like spoken Russian set to music.
- Russian Orthodox bell texture: open 5th drone bass, parallel 5th chord movement, slow-moving choral harmony without leading tone. Boris Godunov's coronation scene, The Great Gate of Kiev.
- Supernatural scales: whole-tone and octatonic fragments for demonic or supernatural moments (Night on Bald Mountain, Boris's hallucination) — before Debussy made them fashionable.
- Programmatic specificity: Pictures at an Exhibition is the paradigm — each piece is a specific portrait, place, or object, described with specific and unique musical material.
