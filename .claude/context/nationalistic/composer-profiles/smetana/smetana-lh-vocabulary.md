# Smetana Left-Hand Vocabulary & Phrase Breathing

Smetana writes **programmatic, narrative music with Czech dance rhythms**. The
left hand is orchestral in conception — full, active, and rhythmically
characterised by the dance underneath whatever is happening.

## LH idiom catalog
1. **Polka oom-pah** — bright, square, duple. The national dance and his default
   energy.
2. **Furiant cross-rhythm** — duple accents across 3/4.
3. **Full sustained chords** under a lyrical melody, orchestral in weight.
4. **Rippling arpeggio depicting water** — the Vltava figure, even and continuous.
5. **Tremolo** for tension and for orchestral transcription.
6. **Repeated chords, insistent**, driving a narrative passage.
7. **Bare octaves for a fate or fanfare motif.**
8. **Drone fifths** for a rustic scene.
9. **Counter-melody in the bass** in the lyrical passages.
10. **March rhythm** — dotted, firm, for the heroic material.
11. **Silence before a narrative turn.**

## Where the idiom changes
At every change of scene. The music is narrative and the texture follows the
story.

## Breathing
Clear sectional boundaries; the texture stops or changes completely between
episodes.

## What to avoid
- **Abstraction.** The music depicts.
- **Thin textures.** Orchestral weight even at the keyboard.
- **Losing the dance.** The Czech rhythms are the national identity.
