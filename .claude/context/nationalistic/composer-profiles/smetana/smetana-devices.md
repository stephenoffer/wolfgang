# Smetana Idiomatic Device Catalog

Companion to `smetana-lh-vocabulary.md`. The organising fact: **programmatic
narrative** — the music tells a specific story, and the form follows the story
rather than an abstract scheme.

## Programmatic devices
1. **Depiction** — water, forest, hunt, dance, castle. Literal and unembarrassed.
2. **The recurring theme as a character or place** — returning transformed as the
   narrative requires.
3. **Scene change by abrupt texture change** rather than by transition.
4. **The rippling water figure** — continuous even semiquavers, rising and falling.
5. **Hunting horns** — open fifths and triadic fanfares.
6. **The village dance episode** — polka or furiant interrupting the narrative.

## Rhythmic devices
7. **Polka** — duple, bright, square.
8. **Furiant** — duple accents across triple metre.
9. **Skočná** — a fast leaping duple dance.
10. **March rhythm** for heroic material.
11. **Syncopation for excitement.**

## Harmonic and melodic devices
12. **Modal folk inflection** — flat sevenths, Lydian fourths.
13. **Third-related modulation** for a scene change.
14. **Long pedal under a rising sequence** to build to a climax.
15. **The broad hymnic theme** for a patriotic or landscape climax.
16. **Melody in octaves at a full statement.**
17. **Diatonic clarity** — the harmony serves the picture.

## Structural devices
18. **Symphonic poem** — one movement, the form dictated by the programme.
19. **Cyclic theme across a set of movements.**
20. **The apotheosis** — the main theme returning grandly at the end.
21. **Sectional construction** with sharp contrasts.

## What to avoid
- **Abstract development.** The programme governs.
- **Understatement.**
- **Losing the Czech rhythmic identity.**
