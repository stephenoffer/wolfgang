# Edward Elgar — Index

**Era:** Late Romantic | **Nationality:** British | **1857–1934**

**Key Works:** Enigma Variations, Cello Concerto in E minor, Violin Concerto Op.61, Symphonies 1–2, The Dream of Gerontius (oratorio), Introduction and Allegro for strings, Pomp and Circumstance Marches

**Style Summary:**
- The English Late Romantic: inhabits the Late Romantic orchestral tradition (large forces, chromatic harmony, emotional directness) with a specifically English pastoral warmth.
- Nobilmente (nobly): Elgar's signature emotional quality — broad, stately, proud but tinged with regret. The nobility is always clouded.
- Sequential builds toward emotional climax: Tchaikovsky's technique applied with English restraint and warmth.
- Inner-voice countermelody: Elgar's textures always have a hidden countermelody in an inner instrument commenting on the main melody.
- Modal English pastoral: flat-7th (Mixolydian) and modal inflections give a rural, historical English quality without folk-music directness.
