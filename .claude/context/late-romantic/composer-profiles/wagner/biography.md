# Richard Wagner — Biography (Late Romantic Period)

**This is the late-romantic profile.** For the full biography covering all periods, see [romantic/composer-profiles/wagner/biography.md](../../../romantic/composer-profiles/wagner/biography.md). This file focuses on the late works (Ring completion, Parsifal) and Wagner's role as the fountainhead of Late Romantic style.

Wagner's late period is where the revolution becomes a religion. The Ring cycle is completed, Bayreuth is built, Parsifal transforms opera into ritual. These works do not merely represent Late Romanticism — they invented it. Every Late Romantic composer from Bruckner to Mahler to Strauss is responding to what Wagner achieved between 1869 and 1882.

## Late Period in Brief

| Year | Location | Event | Musical Significance |
|------|----------|-------|---------------------|
| 1864 | Munich | King Ludwig II rescues Wagner from debt | Royal patronage enables the final vision; Tristan (1865) and Meistersinger (1868) premiered |
| 1869 | Munich | Das Rheingold premiered (against Wagner's wishes) | The Ring enters the world; the public hears the Leitmotif system for the first time |
| 1870 | Munich | Die Walkure premiered | Emotional core of the Ring; the Walkure Ride, Wotan's farewell — the late-romantic orchestra fully revealed |
| 1871 | Bayreuth | Wagner announces the Bayreuth Festival | A theatre built for one composer's vision; the sunken pit; the darkened auditorium |
| 1874 | Bayreuth | Gotterdammerung completed | The Ring finished after 26 years; the most ambitious work in Western music |
| 1876 | Bayreuth | Complete Ring cycle premiered | 4 operas, 16 hours, 120+ players; the festival that changed the world |
| 1877 | Bayreuth | Begins Parsifal | The sacred stage-work; extreme slowness; ppp dominates; redemption through compassion |
| 1882 | Bayreuth | Parsifal premiered | The final masterpiece; communion music; the orchestra as incense |
| 1883 | Venice | Dies, February 13, age 69 | Heart failure; buried at Wahnfried; the aftershock reshapes all music that follows |

## The Late Period as Origin of Late Romanticism

| What Wagner Achieved | What It Made Possible |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Chromatic harmony pushed to tonal dissolution | Mahler's wandering tonality; Strauss's extended tonality; Schoenberg's atonality |
| Orchestra of 120+ with unprecedented dynamic range | Bruckner's massive symphonies; Mahler's thousand-player works; Strauss's tone poems |
| Leitmotif as structural principle | Film scoring's character-theme system; Strauss's thematic metamorphosis |
| Through-composition replacing discrete numbers | All Late Romantic opera; symphonic continuity in concert works |
| Bayreuth as temple of art | The concept of a composer's festival; the audience as worshippers, not socializers |
| Harmonic stasis as expressive device | Bruckner's sustained brass chorales; Parsifal's influence on Debussy's stasis |

## The Bayreuth Revolution

| Element | What Wagner Demanded | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------------------|----------------|
| Hidden orchestra pit | Orchestra sunken below stage; sound reflects off stage ceiling | The orchestral sound becomes ambient, not directional; voices project effortlessly over 120 players |
| Darkened auditorium | No chandelier; audience sits in darkness | The audience cannot socialize; they must attend; art as sacred experience |
| No curtain calls between acts | The drama is not interrupted for applause | Continuous emotional immersion; the act as symphonic whole |
| Double proscenium | Two frames around the stage; creates visual depth | The "mystic gulf" between audience and stage; art separated from life |

## Career Phases (Late Period Only)

| Phase | Period | Character | Defining Works |
|-------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Ring completion | 1869–1874 | Heroic scale; maximum complexity; the Leitmotif web at its densest | Siegfried Act III, Gotterdammerung |
| Bayreuth realization | 1874–1876 | The theatre built; the premiere; the vision made physical | Ring cycle first performance |
| Sacred final period | 1877–1882 | Extreme refinement; slowest tempi; ppp dominates; redemption | Parsifal |
