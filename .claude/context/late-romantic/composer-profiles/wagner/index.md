# Richard Wagner — Index (Late Romantic Period)

**Era:** Late Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1813–1883**

**See:** [romantic/composer-profiles/wagner/index.md](../../../romantic/composer-profiles/wagner/index.md) — full profile. Wagner spans both periods; all composition guidance is in the romantic/ directory.

**Late-period characteristics (Ring Cycle + Parsifal):**
- Leitmotif counterpoint: multiple Leitmotifs heard simultaneously in different instruments
- Harmonic stasis: single chords sustained for 16–32 bars
- Parsifal sound: extremely slow, widely spaced brass chords, high strings pppp, sacred/mystical character
- Maximum complexity of Leitmotif weaving: the orchestra IS the narrative
