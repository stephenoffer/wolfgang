# Richard Strauss — Index

**Era:** Late Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1864–1949**

**Key Works:** Tone poems: Don Juan, Till Eulenspiegel, Also sprach Zarathustra, Don Quixote, Ein Heldenleben, Tod und Verklärung; Operas: Salome, Elektra, Der Rosenkavalier, Ariadne auf Naxos, Arabella; Four Last Songs, Oboe Concerto

**Style Summary:**
- Post-Wagnerian orchestral mastery: 100+ players as a single integrated instrument, ranging from solo violin harmonics to full fff brass.
- Tone poem as cinematic narrative: every major work has a specific extra-musical program (character, concept, autobiography). Music as biography.
- Lush chromatic harmony with tonal clarity: the most chromatic of the Late Romantics who never loses his key. Rich inner voice motion, 9ths and 11ths as default.
- Ironic quotation: heroic arrivals undercut by comic figures; solemn chorales turned into dances.
- Late style (Der Rosenkavalier onward): abandons complexity for autumnal lyricism — long, beautiful melodic lines, warm orchestral texture, chromatic but approachable.
