# Anton Bruckner — Index

**Era:** Late Romantic | **Nationality:** Austrian | **1824–1896**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 4 (Romantic), 7, 8, 9 (unfinished); Mass in D minor, Mass in F minor, Te Deum, Motets (Locus Iste, Os Justi, Virga Jesse)

**Style Summary:**
- Cathedral scale: Bruckner's symphonies are enormous — 60–80 minutes, massive forces, architectural proportions inspired by Gothic cathedrals (he was a devout Catholic and church organist).
- Absolute tutti vs. silence: two states alternating without gradual transitions. From fff full orchestra → one beat of silence → pp chamber texture. No mf.
- The Bruckner rhythm: a specific syncopated pattern (duplet against triplet) in inner voices that creates perpetual rhythmic energy underneath sustained harmonies.
- Chorale brass: the horn/trombone section functions as a cathedral organ — slow, sustained, harmonically full, majestic.
- Very slow harmonic rhythm: single chords sustained for 8–16 bars, especially in slow movements. The chord has enormous weight from duration alone.
