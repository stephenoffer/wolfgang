# Richard Wagner — Index (Romantic Period)

**Era:** Romantic → Late Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1813–1883**

**Key Works:** Flying Dutchman, Tannhäuser, Lohengrin (early), Tristan und Isolde, Die Meistersinger, The Ring Cycle (Das Rheingold, Die Walküre, Siegfried, Götterdämmerung), Parsifal (late)

**Style Summary (Three Periods):**
- **Early (1842–1850):** Flying Dutchman, Tannhäuser, Lohengrin — still uses aria/recitative structure, clearer phrases, more conventional harmony. Grand Romantic opera rather than music-drama.
- **Middle (1856–1868):** Tristan und Isolde, Die Meistersinger — mature style: endless melody, Leitmotif system fully developed, chromatic saturation (Tristan chord), no conventional phrase endings.
- **Late (1869–1882):** Ring Cycle, Parsifal — maximum chromatic ambiguity, slowest harmonic rhythm, most complex Leitmotif weaving. Parsifal: extreme slowness, sacred character, pppp texture.

**Defining contributions:** Leitmotif system, endless melody (no cadences), chromatic harmony as emotional language, orchestra representing the unconscious.
