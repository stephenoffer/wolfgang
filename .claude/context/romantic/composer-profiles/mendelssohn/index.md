# Felix Mendelssohn — Index

**Era:** Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1809–1847**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 3 (Scottish) and 4 (Italian), Violin Concerto in E minor, Piano Concerto No.1, Octet Op.20, String Quartets Op.12/44/80, A Midsummer Night's Dream (incl. Scherzo), Songs Without Words (48 piano pieces), Elijah (oratorio), St. Paul (oratorio)

**Style Summary:**
- The "Classical Romantic": inhabits Classical forms (sonata, rondo, variation) with genuine conviction and craft — not nostalgically but naturally.
- Elfin scherzo texture: his defining innovation — fast, pp, staccato, high register, weightless. No one else sounds like this.
- Bel canto melody in instrumental music: long, arching, vocal, ornamental at cadences.
- Protestant clarity: transparent orchestration, no mud, each instrumental voice audible.
- Barcarolle/nocturne idiom in 6/8: the Songs Without Words define a specific gentle lyricism.
