# Franz Schubert — Index

**Era:** Romantic (early) | **Nationality:** Austrian | **1797–1828**

**Key Works:** Winterreise (song cycle), Die schöne Müllerin, Schwanengesang, "Unfinished" Symphony (No.8), Symphony No.9 "Great C Major", Piano Sonatas D.845/894/958/959/960, String Quintet D.956, String Quartet D.810 "Death and the Maiden", Trout Quintet, Impromptus D.899/935

**Style Summary:**
- The song composer who made everything sing: every Schubert melody could be set to words; every phrase has the shape of a sung line.
- Parallel major-minor shift (the Schubertian flip): C major → C minor → C major with one note changing. No modulation — just light and shadow.
- Third-relation harmony: moves by major 3rd (C→E, C→Ab) without preparation, treating the new key as a different color on the same canvas.
- The divine moment: time stops — sustained chord at ppp, high strings only, no bass, 4–8 bars of transcendence.
- Short life (31 years) but enormous output: over 600 songs, 21 piano sonatas, 9 symphonies, much chamber music.
