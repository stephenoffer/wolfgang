# Sergei Rachmaninoff — Index

**Era:** Romantic (late) | **Nationality:** Russian | **1873–1943**

**Key Works:** Piano Concertos 2–4, Rhapsody on a Theme of Paganini, Symphonies 2–3, The Isle of the Dead, Vocalise, Piano Sonatas 1–2, Études-Tableaux, Preludes (complete), Vespers (All-Night Vigil), The Bells

**Style Summary:**
- Last great Romantic: writing in fully developed late-Romantic language long after Schoenberg's atonal revolution. Not conservative — simply unconvinced.
- Long arching melody (8–16 bars) as the central musical value. The melody is always the point.
- Wide-range piano texture: bass in lowest register, melody in soprano, rapid arpeggiated middle voices simultaneously.
- Subdominant approach to climax: bVI→IV→I (not dominant). The approach from below is more powerful than dominant tension.
- Bell resonance: tonic pedal points, open 5ths, Russian Orthodox liturgical echo throughout.
