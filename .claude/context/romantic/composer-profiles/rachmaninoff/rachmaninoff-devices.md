# Rachmaninoff Idiomatic Device Catalog

Companion to `rachmaninoff-lh-vocabulary.md`. The organising fact: **a long
melody and a long build**. The characteristic shape is an extended lyrical theme
followed by a gradual intensification over many bars to a full-texture climax,
then collapse.

## Melodic devices
1. **The long-breathed theme** — sixteen to thirty-two bars, mostly stepwise,
   arching once. Write the tune first and make it long.
2. **Stepwise melodic motion with occasional expressive leaps.**
3. **Melody doubled in octaves** at the climax.
4. **Sequential rise to a peak** — the same phrase a step higher, three times.
5. **The falling sequence after the peak** — sighs descending.
6. **Melody in the tenor register** under the accompaniment.

## Harmonic devices
7. **Chromatic inner-voice motion** over a slow-moving bass.
8. **Modal mixture** — constant borrowing from the parallel minor.
9. **Extended dominants** — ninths and elevenths held and unresolved.
10. **The augmented sixth at the emotional peak.**
11. **Long dominant pedal under a rising sequence** — the standard build.
12. **Deceptive resolution to delay the tonic.**
13. **Dies Irae quotation** — the four-note plainchant, used as a fate motif
    across many works.

## Structural devices
14. **The long build** — twenty or more bars of continuous intensification.
15. **The collapse** — full texture to a single line immediately after a climax.
16. **The climax at the golden section.**
17. **Cyclic theme across movements.**
18. **The quiet dark ending** — low, `ppp`, after everything.
19. **Bell sonorities** as structural punctuation.
20. **Variation form on a borrowed theme.**

## What to avoid
- **Short phrases.** The style is expansive.
- **A thin climax.** The peak should use the full instrument.
- **Understated emotion.**
- **A bright, high centre.** The register is dark.
