# Johannes Brahms — Index

**Era:** Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1833–1897**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 1–4, Piano Concertos 1–2, Violin Concerto, Double Concerto, German Requiem, Piano Sonatas Op.1/2/5, Intermezzos Op.117/118/119, String Quartets Op.51/67, Clarinet Quintet Op.115

**Style Summary:**
- The conservative Romantic: Classical forms (sonata, variation, passacaglia) inhabited with Romantic harmonic richness. Brahms chose formal rigor when Liszt and Wagner chose form-dissolution.
- Dense, thick textures: inner voices always active, bass register heavy, counterpoint embedded in everything.
- Rhythmic complexity: hemiola at cadences, cross-rhythms (3-against-2), phrases that avoid the downbeat.
- Subdominant gravity: IV→I, flat-VII→I, and bVI as his cadential vocabulary — not dominant resolution.
- Autumnal emotional character: gravity, nostalgia, occasional warmth — never lightness, rarely triumph.
