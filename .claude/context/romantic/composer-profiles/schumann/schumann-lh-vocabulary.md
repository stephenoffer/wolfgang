# Schumann Left-Hand Vocabulary & Phrase Breathing

Schumann's left hand is **restless and syncopated**. Its most characteristic
behaviour is to be off the beat: the bass arrives late, the chords fall between
the melody's notes, and the downbeat is frequently empty. Generated "Schumann" is
usually far too metrically obedient.

## LH idiom catalog

1. **Off-beat chords with an empty downbeat** — `rest_e [C3,E3,G3]e [C3,E3,G3]e
   [C3,E3,G3]e`. The bar has no floor under beat 1. His most recognisable texture.
2. **Syncopated bass** — the bass note tied over from the previous bar, so it is
   never struck on the downbeat.
3. **Broken chord spanning a tenth** — `C2e G2e E3e G2e`, warmer than an octave
   figure.
4. **Inner-voice melody** — the tune in the middle of the texture, played by the
   left hand's thumb, with accompaniment above and below.
5. **Repeated chords, agitated** — `[C3,E3,G3]s ×16` under a declamatory melody.
6. **Contrapuntal bass** — a real second line, from his Bach study.
7. **Widely-spaced sustained chords** for the intimate slow pieces.
8. **Dotted-rhythm march bass** — `C2dq C2e G2dq G2e`, for the character pieces.
9. **Arpeggio crossing above the right hand** — hand crossing as colour.
10. **Bare octaves** for a declamatory opening.
11. **Silence for a beat mid-phrase** — the texture stops and restarts.

## Where the idiom changes
At each character piece in a cycle — the pieces are short and each has its own
texture, which is the form.

## Breathing
Schumann's phrases are short and his pieces are short. Breathing happens between
character pieces more than inside them.

## What to avoid
- **Metrical regularity.** The syncopation is the style.
- **Long forms.** Schumann's strength is the miniature; a sprawling movement is
  not idiomatic.
- **Clear textural separation.** The middle register is where he lives, and the
  hands overlap constantly.
