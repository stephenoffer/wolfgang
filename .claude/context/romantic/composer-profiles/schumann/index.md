# Robert Schumann — Index

**Era:** Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1810–1856**

**Key Works:** Piano Concerto Op.54, Symphonies 1–4, Carnaval Op.9, Kinderszenen Op.15, Kreisleriana Op.16, Fantasie Op.17, Dichterliebe (song cycle), Frauenliebe und -leben, String Quartet Op.41, Piano Quintet Op.44

**Style Summary:**
- The literary Romantic: every Schumann piece has a hidden narrative or character — Florestan (extrovert) or Eusebius (introvert), or both simultaneously.
- Hidden inner melody: the main tune is not on top — it lives in the tenor or an inner voice while the soprano plays accompaniment. Finding it is the reward.
- Rhythmic displacement: phrases begin on weak beats, ties across barlines create "leaning" melody, hemiola in 6/8.
- Sudden tonal leaps without preparation: E major → C major, or C minor → A major — not modulation but arrival.
- Dense piano texture as interior monologue: melody, countermelody, bass, inner harmony all simultaneously.
