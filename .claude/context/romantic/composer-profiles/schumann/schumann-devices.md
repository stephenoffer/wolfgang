# Schumann Idiomatic Device Catalog

Companion to `schumann-lh-vocabulary.md`. The organising fact: Schumann thinks in
**character pieces** — short, sharply characterised movements collected into
cycles. A single long argument is not his form.

## Rhythmic devices

1. **Persistent syncopation** — the melody or the accompaniment consistently off
   the beat for a whole piece.
2. **Empty downbeat** — beat 1 with nothing on it, repeatedly.
3. **Metric ambiguity** — the notated metre and the heard metre disagreeing for
   long stretches.
4. **Dotted march rhythm** as a character marker.
5. **Cross-rhythm between the hands.**

## Melodic devices

6. **The motto cipher** — a short pitch sequence used as a signature and woven
   through a cycle (ASCH, or a name spelled in notes).
7. **Inner-voice melody** — the tune buried in the middle of the texture.
8. **Fragmented, aphoristic phrases** — a piece can be sixteen bars long and
   complete.
9. **The sighing appoggiatura**, densely used.
10. **Melody in dialogue between the hands** — a phrase begun by one and finished
    by the other.

## Harmonic devices

11. **Chromatic inner voices** over a static bass.
12. **Deceptive resolutions** used repeatedly so the tonic is delayed.
13. **Modal mixture** for sudden shadow.
14. **Sequences of dominant sevenths** sliding chromatically.
15. **Ending on an unresolved or ambiguous harmony** — several of the character
    pieces simply stop.

## Structural devices

16. **The cycle** — eight to twenty short pieces, each characterised, unified by a
    motto or a key scheme.
17. **Contrasting alter egos** — two opposed characters (impetuous / dreamy)
    alternating across a set. Florestan and Eusebius.
18. **Quotation** — a phrase from another work or another movement appearing
    briefly.
19. **The unexpected ending** — a piece that stops mid-gesture.
20. **Literary titles and programmes** driving the character of each piece.

## What to avoid
- **Metrical squareness.**
- **A long developmental movement.** The miniature is the idiom.
- **Bright, clear textures.** Schumann's writing is dense and middle-register.
- **Resolving everything.** Ambiguity is part of the voice.
