# Frédéric Chopin — Index

**Era:** Romantic | **Nationality:** Polish-French | **1810–1849**

**Key Works:** 21 Nocturnes, 27 Études, 4 Ballades, 4 Scherzi, 3 Piano Sonatas, 2 Piano Concertos, 58 Mazurkas, 16 Polonaises, 24 Preludes, Barcarolle, Berceuse, Fantasie

**Style Summary:** Chopin wrote almost exclusively for piano and made the piano's capabilities his entire compositional language. The nocturne bass (LH arpeggiated broken octave) beneath a singing RH melody is his primary texture; the étude form his laboratory for developing idiomatic figuration; the polonaise and mazurka his connection to Polish national identity. His harmony is chromatic and personal — enharmonic modulations, Neapolitan chords, augmented sixths appear not as theoretical exercises but as expressions of longing, memory, or melancholy. His melodies breathe and hesitate with rubato.
