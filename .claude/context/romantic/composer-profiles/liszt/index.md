# Franz Liszt — Index

**Era:** Romantic | **Nationality:** Hungarian | **1811–1886**

**Key Works:** Piano Sonata in B minor, 19 Hungarian Rhapsodies, 12 Symphonic Poems (Les Préludes, Mazeppa), Faust Symphony, Dante Symphony, Transcendental Études, Piano Concertos 1–2, Via Crucis (late works)

**Style Summary:**
- Inventor of the symphonic poem and thematic transformation — one theme undergoes metamorphosis across an entire work.
- Virtuosic piano writing that treats the instrument as a full orchestra: bass octaves, soprano melody, rapid inner voices simultaneously.
- Chromatic harmonic language approaching Wagner: augmented chords, enharmonic pivots, extended tonal ambiguity.
- Three-period arc: early virtuosity (Hungarian Rhapsodies, Transcendental Études) → middle symphonism (tone poems, Faust Symphony) → late mysticism (Via Crucis — spare, chromatic, anticipating 20th century).
- Hungarian verbunkos idiom: augmented 2nds, dotted rhythms, rapid ornaments.
