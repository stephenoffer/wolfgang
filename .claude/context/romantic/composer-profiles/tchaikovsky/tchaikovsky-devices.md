# Tchaikovsky Idiomatic Device Catalog

Companion to `tchaikovsky-lh-vocabulary.md`. The organising fact: **melody is
primary and emotion is explicit.** The tunes are long, singable and repeated, and
the structure exists to present and intensify them.

## Melodic devices
1. **The long singing theme** — sixteen bars or more, arching once, immediately
   memorable. Write the tune first.
2. **Sequential intensification** — the theme repeated a step higher, louder,
   fuller, two or three times to a climax.
3. **The descending scale as a melodic idea** — simple and direct.
4. **Melody doubled in octaves** at the climax.
5. **The falling sequence of sighs** after a peak.
6. **Repeated-note anticipation** before a long melodic note.

## Harmonic devices
7. **Descending chromatic bass** under a static or rising melody. His most
   reliable intensifier.
8. **Modal mixture** — major and minor of the same tonic alternating, often within
   a phrase.
9. **The augmented sixth at an emotional peak.**
10. **Long dominant pedal** under a rising sequence.
11. **Abrupt shift to a remote key** for a new emotional region.
12. **Full diatonic harmony** — the vocabulary is not complex; the intensity comes
    from register, dynamics and repetition.

## Structural devices
13. **Sonata form organised by emotional contrast** rather than by tonal argument.
14. **The fate motif** — a short, stark idea returning across movements to
    interrupt.
15. **Ballet-derived dance forms** — waltz, march, characteristic dance.
16. **The climax placed at the golden section**, prepared by a long build.
17. **Sudden collapse after a climax** — full texture to a single line.
18. **The quiet, dark ending** — a movement ending `ppp` in the low register.
19. **Extended, repetitive coda** driving a final assertion home.

## What to avoid
- **Understated emotion.** The style is explicit.
- **Contrapuntal density.** The texture is homophonic and orchestral.
- **A short or fragmented theme.** The long tune is the point.
- **Harmonic complexity for its own sake.**
