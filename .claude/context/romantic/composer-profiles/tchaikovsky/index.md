# Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky — Index

**Era:** Romantic | **Nationality:** Russian | **1840–1893**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 4–6 (Pathétique), Piano Concerto No.1, Violin Concerto, Ballets (Swan Lake, Sleeping Beauty, The Nutcracker), String Quartet No.1, Piano Trio Op.50, Romeo and Juliet (fantasy overture), 1812 Overture

**Style Summary:**
- Emotional directness and unashamed scale: Tchaikovsky wears his heart on his sleeve. The climaxes are always fully committed — no restraint at the peak.
- Long arching melodies with a built-in arch: begin low, rise to a peak over 8–16 bars, descend. The peak is the emotional goal; everything else is approach or aftermath.
- Sequential escalation as primary emotional engine: repeat a 2-bar gesture a step higher 3–4 times, each time louder, until the climax arrives inevitably.
- Russian modal color within tonal framework: bVI, Phrygian bII→I, Dorian raised 6th — not modulation, just Russian flavor.
- Waltz rhythm pervades everything: even tragedy has a waltz underneath.
