# George Frideric Handel — Index

**Era:** Baroque | **Nationality:** German-British | **1685–1759**

**Key Works:** Messiah, Water Music, Music for the Royal Fireworks, Giulio Cesare, Saul, Israel in Egypt, Alcina, Twelve Grand Concertos Op. 6

**Style Summary:** The supreme dramatist of Baroque music. Where Bach builds cathedrals, Handel fills stadiums. His music is extrovert, grand, immediately communicative — built on memorable themes that state themselves in four bars and are not forgotten. He synthesized German counterpoint, Italian opera, and English choral tradition into a public art form. His oratorios, written after his opera career collapsed, invented a new genre that appealed to mass audiences without sacrificing musical substance.
