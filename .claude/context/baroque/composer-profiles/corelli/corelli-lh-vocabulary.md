# Corelli Bass Vocabulary & Phrase Breathing

Corelli is the trio sonata: two violins over a continuo bass. The bass is not an
accompaniment figure — it is the third voice, and in the fast movements it
imitates the violins as an equal. What makes the style recognisable is its
extraordinary *regularity*: the harmony moves at a steady rate, the sequences are
exact, and the suspensions arrive on schedule. Generated "Baroque" is usually too
irregular for Corelli, not too regular.

Shorthand reminder: `//` for two voices in one hand. A trio texture needs three
real lines.

## Idiom catalog

1. **Walking quarters through the harmony** — `D3q A2q Bb2q F2q G2q D2q Eb2q A2q`.
   Steady, mostly by step or by fourth, one note per chord. The default and the
   sound of the style.

2. **Circle of fifths, exact** — the bass falling a fifth and rising a fourth,
   four to eight chords in a row: `D3q G2q C3q F2q Bb2q E2q A2q D2q`. Corelli
   uses longer circles than anyone before him, and the exactness is the point.

3. **Suspension chain (the 7-6 or 4-3 chain)** — the upper voices suspend in
   alternation while the bass walks down by step. `D3q C3q Bb2q A2q` under
   suspending violins. This is the single most Corellian texture there is.

4. **Pedal on the dominant** — `A2w` held for four to eight bars before a final
   cadence, with the violins intensifying above.

5. **Repeated notes for insistence** — `D3e ×8` under a sequential passage.

6. **Bass imitating the violins** — in the fugal movements the bass takes the
   subject at the octave below. The trio sonata's fast movements are usually
   fugal and the bass must participate.

7. **Descending tetrachord ground** — `D3h C3h Bb2h A2h`, repeated. Used for the
   slow movements and the chaconne-like finales.

8. **Long notes under a solo passage** — whole notes while a violin ornaments
   freely above. The slow-movement texture.

9. **Cadential 4-3 with the bass falling a fifth** — `A2h D2h` under a suspended
   fourth resolving. Every phrase ends this way; that is not monotony, it is the
   period's punctuation.

10. **Sequences of one-bar units** — the same bar transposed down a step or a
    third, three or four times. Corelli's principal method of continuation, and
    the transposition should be exact.

11. **Silence for one beat before a cadence** — the whole texture stops, then the
    cadence lands. Sparing and effective.

## Regularity is the style

Three things generated Baroque usually gets wrong about Corelli:

- **Harmonic rhythm is even.** One chord per beat, or per bar, and it stays that
  way for a whole section. Irregular harmonic rhythm sounds later.
- **Sequences are exact.** Transpose the whole unit; do not vary it.
- **Phrases are built from repeated units**, not from contrasting ideas. A
  movement can be one figure sequenced and cadenced for forty bars.

## The slow-fast-slow-fast shape

A church sonata alternates: slow (suspensions over a walking bass), fast (fugal,
bass imitating), slow (usually shorter, often in the relative key), fast (dance-
like, often triple time). If a piece in this style has one tempo throughout, the
form is missing.

## Ornamentation

The written slow-movement line is a skeleton; the performer ornaments it. If
writing out the ornamentation, use passing notes, trills at cadences, and turns
around long notes — never virtuoso figuration, which belongs to Vivaldi and after.

## Breathing

At every cadence, and cadences come every four to eight bars with great
regularity. The bass rests briefly; the violins overlap into the next phrase. That
overlap is what keeps the music moving through its very regular punctuation.

## What to avoid

- **A bass that only holds roots.** It walks, it imitates, it sequences.
- **Irregular phrase lengths.** Corelli is squarer than almost anyone.
- **Chromaticism.** The vocabulary is diatonic with secondary dominants inside
  sequences. Remote keys are later.
- **Dynamics beyond terraced echo.** A repeated phrase softer is the whole
  dynamic vocabulary.
- **Alberti or any keyboard-figuration idiom.** This is string writing.
