# Antonio Vivaldi — Index

**Era:** Baroque | **Nationality:** Italian | **1678–1741**

**Key Works:** The Four Seasons, L'estro armonico, Gloria in D, La stravaganza, Orlando furioso, Juditha triumphans, ~500 concertos total

**Style Summary:** The architect of the solo concerto. Vivaldi codified the three-movement fast-slow-fast concerto form and the ritornello principle — the orchestra's punctuation framing the soloist's elaborations — which shaped every concerto for 200 years. His music is brilliant, immediate, rhythmically driving, harmonically clear but inventive. The violin was his instrument and his thinking: everything is shaped by what the violin can do.
