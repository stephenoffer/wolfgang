# Johann Sebastian Bach — Index

**Era:** Baroque | **Nationality:** German | **1685–1750**

**Key Works:** Well-Tempered Clavier (I & II), Mass in B Minor, St Matthew Passion, Brandenburg Concertos, Goldberg Variations, Art of Fugue, Six Cello Suites, Partitas for solo violin

**Style Summary:** The supreme contrapuntist of Western music. Bach synthesized the German, Italian, and French traditions into an architecture of interlocking voices where every note serves both melodic and harmonic functions simultaneously. His music thinks in four independent lines at once. Melody is generated by harmonic logic; harmony is generated by voice-leading logic; rhythm is generated by the melodic shapes.
