# Maurice Ravel — Index

**Era:** Impressionist | **Nationality:** French | **1875–1937**

**Key Works:** Boléro, Piano Concerto in G, Piano Concerto for Left Hand, La valse, Daphnis et Chloé (ballet), String Quartet, Piano Trio, Sonatine, Miroirs, Gaspard de la Nuit (piano), Mother Goose (orchestral), Tzigane (violin)

**Style Summary:**
- Precision and clarity distinguish Ravel from Debussy: where Debussy dissolves, Ravel crystallizes. Every voice is clean, every ornament exact, every texture stratified.
- Mechanical regularity as aesthetic: Boléro is the paradigm — exact repetition for 15 minutes, the regularity itself becoming hypnotic.
- Bitonality and polytonal color: two keys sounding simultaneously, creating specific harmonic flavors neither key could produce alone.
- Spanish/Basque/Iberian color: Ravel was Basque by heritage. Spanish dance rhythms, modal Iberian scales, guitar-like textures.
- Textural stratification: melody in one layer, accompaniment in another, bass in a third — each layer distinct and audible.
