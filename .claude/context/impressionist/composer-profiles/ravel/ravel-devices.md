# Ravel Idiomatic Device Catalog

Companion to `ravel-lh-vocabulary.md`. The organising fact: **Impressionist
harmony inside Classical forms, executed with mechanical precision.** Every
comparison with Debussy turns on exactness.

## Harmonic devices
1. **Added-note and extended chords** — ninths, elevenths, added sixths, used as
   stable sonorities.
2. **Planing**, but in strict rhythm.
3. **Modal writing** — Dorian, Phrygian, and the Spanish Phrygian with its major
   third.
4. **Bitonal touches** — a melody in one key over an ostinato in another.
5. **Unresolved appoggiaturas** left standing as colour.
6. **Whole-tone and octatonic passages** as transitional colour.
7. **A functional bass under non-functional harmony** — the progression still
   moves somewhere, unlike Debussy.

## Rhythmic and textural devices
8. **Exact ostinato** — the same figure, unvaried, accumulating.
9. **Mechanical repetition as a form** — a single crescendo over an unchanging
   pattern.
10. **Dance rhythms** — waltz, habanera, bolero, forlane, each precisely
    characterised.
11. **Clockwork textures** — music-box, automaton, precise and glittering.
12. **Extreme register contrast** with a deliberate empty middle.
13. **Virtuoso cascades** — glissandi, rapid parallel chords, cross-hand leaps.
14. **Orchestral thinking at the keyboard** — each layer is a different
    instrument.

## Structural devices
15. **Classical forms** — sonata, rondo, variation, suite of dances. Clearly
    articulated.
16. **The single long crescendo** as an entire movement's shape.
17. **Neoclassical pastiche** — an eighteenth-century form with twentieth-century
    harmony.
18. **The tombeau** — a suite of baroque dances as memorial.
19. **A quiet, exact ending** — precise rather than evaporating.
20. **Orchestration as composition** — the colour changes are structural.

## What to avoid
- **Vagueness.** Everything is exact.
- **Formal looseness.** The frames are Classical.
- **Excessive rubato.** The pulse holds.
- **Confusing him with Debussy.** Same harmony, opposite temperament.
