# Erik Satie — Index

**Era:** Impressionist / Proto-Minimalist | **Nationality:** French | **1866–1925**

**Key Works:** Gymnopédies (3), Gnossiennes (6), Trois Morceaux en forme de poire, Vexations (repeated 840 times), Socrate (symphonic drama), Sports et Divertissements, Parade (ballet with Cocteau/Picasso)

**Style Summary:**
- Anti-Romantic, anti-Impressionist: Satie rejected both the emotional excess of Romanticism and the sensory luxury of Impressionism. His music is bare, ironic, and deliberately simple.
- "Furniture music" (musique d'ameublement): music meant to exist as sonic wallpaper, not drama. Repetition without development or arrival.
- Parallel chord movement: moves chords in parallel (forbidden by Classical harmony rules) — not as error but as aesthetic choice. The chords don't function; they are colors.
- Extreme simplicity: melody + simple accompaniment, no ornamentation, no development. What remains is not plain — it is essence.
- Sardonic titles as artistic statement: "Three Pieces in the Shape of a Pear," "Genuine Flabby Preludes (for a Dog)." The humor IS the meaning.
