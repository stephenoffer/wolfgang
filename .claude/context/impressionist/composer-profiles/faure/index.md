# Gabriel Fauré — Index

**Era:** Impressionist / Late Romantic | **Nationality:** French | **1845–1924**

**Key Works:** Requiem Op.48, Piano Quartets Op.15/45, Violin Sonatas Op.13/108, Cello Sonatas Op.109/117, Nocturnes (piano, 13), Barcarolles (piano, 13), Pelléas et Mélisande Suite, La Bonne Chanson (song cycle), Dolly Suite

**Style Summary:**
- The intimate Frenchman: Fauré's music rarely exceeds mf. The emotional range is warm, slightly melancholy, always private rather than public.
- Modal harmony within tonal framework: Dorian, Mixolydian, and Lydian inflections within clearly tonal writing. The tonic "slips" slightly — familiar but subtly foreign.
- Gentle rhythmic haze: flowing triplet or eighth-note accompaniment that cushions the beat without emphasizing it.
- Long melody with one modal inflection: 16-bar melody, diatonic and lovely, with one unexpected Dorian or Mixolydian note that makes it distinctively Fauré.
- Avoided cadences: phrases end on deceptive cadences or simply continue. The music flows without punctuation.
