# Claude Debussy — Index

**Era:** Impressionist | **Nationality:** French | **1862–1918**

**Key Works:** Préludes Books 1–2 (piano), Images (piano), Children's Corner, La Mer (orchestra), Nocturnes (orchestra), Pelléas et Mélisande (opera), String Quartet, Violin Sonata, Cello Sonata, Syrinx (solo flute)

**Style Summary:**
- Dissolved the 19th-century harmonic system: no functional progressions, no tension-resolution, chords as colors rather than functions.
- Parallel chord movement: chords move in parallel (all voices same direction, same interval) — the Classical "error" made into an aesthetic.
- Pentatonic and whole-tone surfaces: scales without leading tones, without semitone tension. The music floats.
- Texture as primary parameter: the "what" is less important than the "how it's scored." The color of a sound IS the music.
- Static, non-goal-directed harmony: the music doesn't "go" anywhere — it exists in a state, shimmers, changes color.
