# John Williams — Index

**Era:** Film Score | **Nationality:** American | **born 1932**

**Key Works:** Star Wars, Jaws, Close Encounters, Raiders of the Lost Ark, E.T., Schindler's List, Jurassic Park, Saving Private Ryan, Harry Potter, Memoirs of a Geisha, Lincoln, The Post; also concert music: Violin Concerto, Cello Concerto, Flute Concerto

**Style Summary:**
- The last great film composer in the Romantic tradition: Williams writes for live symphony orchestra with the full command of a Late Romantic master (Holst, Korngold, Strauss).
- Wagnerian Leitmotif system in cinema: each character, object, concept, or relationship has a specific musical tag. These tags appear, combine, and transform as the narrative develops. The Leitmotif IS the storytelling.
- Heroic fanfare idiom: brass in unison or close harmony, dotted rhythms, major key, fff. Star Wars, Indiana Jones, Imperial March — all built on this archetype.
- Lyrical "heart" themes: contrasting with the fanfares — intimate, singable themes for emotional centers (Schindler's List solo violin, E.T. bicycle scene, Hedwig's Theme).
- Characteristic harmonic fingerprints: bVI→I (magical/wonder), Lydian raised 4th (discovery), modal mixture for ominous effect.
