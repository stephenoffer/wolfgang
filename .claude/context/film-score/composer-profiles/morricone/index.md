# Ennio Morricone — Index

**Era:** Film Score | **Nationality:** Italian | **1928–2020**

**Key Works:** The Good the Bad and the Ugly, Once Upon a Time in the West, Once Upon a Time in America, The Mission, Cinema Paradiso, The Untouchables, Nuovo Cinema Paradiso, Eight Bucks, The Hateful Eight; also concert music: Cantata per l'Europa, Voci dal Silenzio

**Style Summary:**
- The eclectic master: Morricone is deliberately unpredictable. His instrument choices are "wrong" (harmonica, ocarina, jaw harp, whistling as primary melodic voice), his textures are unexpected, his emotional contrasts are extreme.
- Simple melody + complex texture: the primary melody is always simple enough to hum. The orchestral background may be complex, unusual, or dissonant — the melody anchors it.
- Spaghetti Western sonic world: wide-open harmonic space (open 5ths, sustained single notes), structural silences (2–4 bars of nothing), specific sound objects (twangy guitar, distant trumpet, wind).
- Modal/folk melodic language: Italian folk music, Spanish/Latin American inflections, modal scales (Dorian, Phrygian) — timeless, ancient quality, not Hollywood.
- Countermelody architecture: 2–3 simultaneous singable melodic layers all independent. The Good the Bad and the Ugly main theme has melody, bass countermelody, and rhythmic ostinato simultaneously.
