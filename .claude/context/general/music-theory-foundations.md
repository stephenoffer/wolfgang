# Music Theory Foundations

## Scales & Intervals

### Major Scale Intervals
`W W H W W W H` (W=whole step, H=half step)

### Minor Scale Forms

| Form | Intervals (from root) | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Natural minor | 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 | W H W W H W W |
| Harmonic minor | 1 2 b3 4 5 b6 7 | W H W W H A2 H |
| Melodic minor (asc) | 1 2 b3 4 5 6 7 | W H W W W W H |
| Melodic minor (desc) | = natural minor descending | W W H W W H W |

### Modes (built on C major)

| Mode | Starting degree | Defining interval | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ionian (I) | C | natural 4, natural 7 | Bright, standard major |
| Dorian (ii) | D | b3, natural 6 | Minor with bright 6th |
| Phrygian (iii) | E | b2, b3 | Dark, Spanish flavor |
| Lydian (IV) | F | #4 | Bright, ethereal |
| Mixolydian (V) | G | b7 | Major with flat 7, dominant |
| Aeolian (vi) | A | b3, b6, b7 | Natural minor |
| Locrian (vii) | B | b2, b5 | Diminished, unstable |

## Interval Reference

| Interval | Semitones | Quality | Common usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unison | 0 | Perfect | Doubling, emphasis |
| Minor 2nd | 1 | Dissonant | Tension, chromaticism |
| Major 2nd | 2 | Mild dissonance | Stepwise motion, suspensions |
| Minor 3rd | 3 | Consonant (imperfect) | Minor triads, sadness |
| Major 3rd | 4 | Consonant (imperfect) | Major triads, brightness |
| Perfect 4th | 5 | Perfect (context-dep.) | Suspensions; consonant below bass only if supported |
| Tritone | 6 | Dissonant | Dominant tension, resolution driver |
| Perfect 5th | 7 | Perfect | Root-fifth stability, open sound |
| Minor 6th | 8 | Consonant (imperfect) | First-inversion minor triad |
| Major 6th | 9 | Consonant (imperfect) | First-inversion major triad |
| Minor 7th | 10 | Dissonant | Dominant 7ths, mixolydian color |
| Major 7th | 11 | Dissonant | Leading tone tension, jazz color |
| Octave | 12 | Perfect | Doubling, reinforcement |

**Consonance hierarchy:** P1/P8 > P5 > 3rds/6ths > P4 (above bass = dissonant in CP) > 2nds/7ths/tritone

## Chord Construction

### Triads

| Type | Intervals | Semitones | Example (C root) | Symbol |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Major | R-M3-P5 | 0-4-7 | C E G | C |
| Minor | R-m3-P5 | 0-3-7 | C Eb G | Cm, c |
| Diminished | R-m3-d5 | 0-3-6 | C Eb Gb | Cdim, c° |
| Augmented | R-M3-A5 | 0-4-8 | C E G# | Caug, C+ |

### Seventh Chords

| Type | Intervals | Semitones | Example (C) | Symbol | Typical function |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Major 7th | R-M3-P5-M7 | 0-4-7-11 | C E G B | Cmaj7, CM7 | Tonic (I) |
| Dominant 7th | R-M3-P5-m7 | 0-4-7-10 | C E G Bb | C7 | Dominant (V) |
| Minor 7th | R-m3-P5-m7 | 0-3-7-10 | C Eb G Bb | Cm7, Cmin7 | Supertonic (ii) |
| Half-dim 7th | R-m3-d5-m7 | 0-3-6-10 | C Eb Gb Bb | Cm7b5, Co/7 | Leading-tone (viio in minor) |
| Diminished 7th | R-m3-d5-dd7 | 0-3-6-9 | C Eb Gb Bbb | Cdim7, Co7 | viio7 in minor, chromatic passing |
| Minor-major 7th | R-m3-P5-M7 | 0-3-7-11 | C Eb G B | CmM7 | i in harmonic minor |

### Extended Chords (jazz / late-Romantic)

| Chord | Construction | Notes | Key point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9th | 7th chord + M9 (or m9) | C E G Bb D | Dom9 most common; m9 = altered |
| 11th | 9th chord + P11 | C E G Bb D F | In practice, 3rd often omitted (clashes w/ 11) |
| 13th | 11th chord + M13 | C E G Bb D F A | 11th often omitted; 13th = added 6th above 7th |

## Chord Symbol Conventions

### Roman Numeral Analysis

| Degree | Major key | Minor key (harmonic) |
|---|---|---|
| I / i | I (major) | i (minor) |
| II / ii | ii (minor) | ii° (diminished) |
| III / III+ | iii (minor) | III+ (augmented) |
| IV / iv | IV (major) | iv (minor) |
| V | V (major) | V (major — raised 7th) |
| VI / vi | vi (minor) | VI (major) |
| VII / vii° | vii° (diminished) | vii° (diminished) |

**Conventions:** Uppercase = major triad. Lowercase = minor triad. ° = diminished. + = augmented.
**Inversions:** I6 = first inversion, I64 = second inversion. V65, V43, V42 = seventh chord inversions.
**Applied chords:** V/V = secondary dominant of V. vii°7/ii = secondary leading-tone seventh of ii.

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|

## Circle of Fifths (Clockwise from C)

```
        C (0)
    F (1b)   G (1#)
  Bb (2b)       D (2#)
 Eb (3b)         A (3#)
  Ab (4b)       E (4#)
    Db (5b)   B (5#)
      Gb/F# (6b/6#)
```

**Key relationships:**
- Adjacent keys differ by one accidental (closely related)
- Opposite keys are tritone apart (maximally distant)
- Relative major/minor share a key signature (3 semitones apart)
- Parallel major/minor share a tonic (differ by 3 accidentals)

**Common modulation targets from tonic I:**
- V (dominant) — most natural
- IV (subdominant) — second most natural
- vi (relative minor) — smooth, shared tones
- iii (mediant) — Romantic-era color
- bVI, bIII (chromatic mediants) — dramatic color shift
