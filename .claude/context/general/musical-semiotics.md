# Musical Semiotics — Why Sounds Mean Things

## Interval Expressiveness

| Interval | Semitones | Affect | Why It Works | Repertoire Example |
|----------|-----------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Unison | 0 | Unity, resolve, blankness | No tension; total agreement | Gregorian chant openings |
| Minor 2nd | 1 | Pain, intimacy, dread | Maximum friction; adjacent pitches grind | Jaws motif, Tristan approach |
| Major 2nd | 2 | Stepping, searching, gentle tension | Close but not grinding; implies continuation | Debussy stepwise motion |
| Minor 3rd | 3 | Sadness, tenderness, folk | Defines minor mode; softened brightness | Beethoven "Moonlight" theme |
| Major 3rd | 4 | Warmth, contentment, clarity | Defines major mode; stable consonance | "Morning" from Peer Gynt |
| Perfect 4th | 5 | Questioning, openness, call | Ambiguous — not yet resolved to 3rd | "Here Comes the Bride" |
| Tritone | 6 | Evil, tension, the forbidden | Maximum tonal instability; divides octave equally | Saint-Saëns Danse Macabre |
| Perfect 5th | 7 | Stability, power, the open horizon | Strongest overtone consonance after octave | R. Strauss Also sprach opening |
| Minor 6th | 8 | Yearning, reaching, anguish | Wide enough to feel stretched; minor quality darkens | Love theme reach upward |
| Major 6th | 9 | Nobility, warmth, lyric sweep | Wide and bright; vocal-range leap | "My Bonnie Lies Over the Ocean" |
| Minor 7th | 10 | Tension seeking resolution, blues | Dominant pull; wants to collapse inward | Bernstein "Somewhere" (opening) |
| Major 7th | 11 | Brightness with edge, longing | Nearly an octave but not — tantalizing nearness | Ravel, jazz voicings |
| Octave | 12 | Transcendence, reinforcement, power | Perfect doubling; same note, new register | Fanfares, Mahler brass |

## Harmonic Affect

### Chord Quality

| Chord Type | Affect | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Major triad | Stability, joy, resolution | Aligns with overtone series; acoustically natural |
| Minor triad | Sadness, introspection | Lowered 3rd darkens the natural overtone |
| Diminished | Anxiety, instability | Two stacked minor 3rds; no stable interval |
| Augmented | Otherworldly, floating | Symmetrical; no tonal anchor point |
| Dominant 7th | Drive, expectation | Tritone inside demands resolution |
| Major 7th | Lushness, sophistication | Near-dissonance softened by major context |
| Minor 7th | Warmth with motion | Minor color + dominant energy |
| Diminished 7th | Crisis, panic | Fully symmetrical; can resolve anywhere |
| Suspended | Anticipation, open | Missing 3rd = missing identity; waits |
| Quartal | Modernity, ambiguity | No triadic function; floating stacks |
| Cluster | Shock, density, chaos | All adjacent pitches; maximum friction |
| Power chord (R-5) | Raw force, archaic | No 3rd = no mode; primal |

### Progression Affect

| Progression | Affect | Why |
|------------|--------|-----|
| V–I | Resolution, arrival | Dominant tension released to tonic |
| IV–I (plagal) | Sacred, gentle closure | Subdominant is softer than dominant |
| i–bVI–bVII–i | Heroic defiance | Borrowed chords lift minor without resolving to major |
| I–vi–IV–V | Nostalgia, comfort | Circle of common tones; gentle motion |
| bVI–bVII–I | Triumph from darkness | Chromatic ascent arrives at major tonic |
| i–iv–v–i | Hopelessness, trapped | All minor; no dominant push toward light |
| I–bVI | Sudden wonder/shock | Chromatic mediant; shared tone but distant key |
| V/V–V–I | Intensified arrival | Double pull magnifies resolution |
| i–V–bVI–bIII | Lament, descent | Falling pattern; bass drops by step |
| Chromatic ascent | Rising tension/ecstasy | Each semitone step adds urgency |

## Melodic Gesture Archetypes

| Gesture | Affect | Mechanism | ABC Example |
|---------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Rising line | Aspiration, growth, hope | Effort against gravity; reaching | `C D E F G A B c` |
| Falling line | Resignation, grief, settling | Yielding to gravity; release | `c B A G F E D C` |
| Arch (rise-fall) | Narrative completion, longing | Journey out and return | `C E G c G E C` |
| Inverted arch | Descent into depths, then escape | Crisis and recovery | `c A F C, F A c` |
| Circling/oscillation | Obsession, meditation, stasis | Trapped in narrow orbit | `E F E D E F E D` |
| Leap then step back | Yearning; reaching then retreating | Desire restrained | `C G F E D` |
| Ascending sequence | Building intensity, insistence | Repetition at higher pitch = escalation | `C D E \| D E F \| E F G` |
| Descending sequence | Fading, unwinding, lament | Pattern sinks; inevitability | `G F E \| F E D \| E D C` |
| Static/repeated note | Fate, urgency, monotony | No motion = pressure or emptiness | `G G G G G G G G` |
| Wide leaps | Drama, instability, passion | Large intervals = emotional extremes | `C, G E c` |

## Rhythmic Semiotics

| Pattern | Affect | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Dotted rhythm (long-short) | Nobility, march, ceremony | French overture tradition; measured pomp |
| Even quarter notes | Steadiness, inevitability, march | Heartbeat regularity; clockwork |
| Triplets | Flow, grace, pastoral | Groups of 3 = rolling, less angular |
| Syncopation | Unease, vitality, rebellion | Off-beat = against expectation |
| Ostinato | Fate, obsession, machinery | Unchanging = inescapable force |
| Hemiola | Tension, arrival, wit | Metric clash creates anticipation |
| Silence/rest | Shock, grief, breath | Absence = presence of meaning |
| Accelerando pattern | Urgency, excitement, panic | Faster = more energy / less control |
| Augmented rhythm | Grandeur, weight, reflection | Slower = heavier / more important |
| Rubato/free | Intimacy, spontaneity, speech | Non-metric = personal, human |
| Cross-rhythm | Conflict, complexity, dual nature | Two patterns clash = two forces |

## Timbral Affect

### Instrument Families

| Family | Core Affect | Why |
|--------|------------|-----|
| Strings | Emotion, warmth, the human voice | Continuous bow = sustained singing; closest to vocal quality |
| Woodwinds | Nature, innocence, color | Breath-driven; individual character per instrument |
| Brass | Power, authority, the heroic | Volume + projection; military/ceremonial history |
| Percussion | Primal, rhythm, the body | Oldest instruments; pulse = heartbeat |
| Harp/keyboard | Ethereal, transitional, heavenly | Plucked/struck = celestial arpeggiation |

### Individual Instruments

| Instrument | Semiotic Weight | Typical Association |
|-----------|----------------|---------------------|
| Solo violin | The individual soul, protagonist | Passion, virtuosity, loneliness |
| Cello | Deep emotion, gravitas | Elegy, mature love, nobility |
| Viola | Inner voice, mystery | The unspoken, introspection |
| Solo flute | Innocence, nature, spirit | Pastoral, birdsong, the ethereal |
| Oboe | Pastoral longing, plaintive cry | Nostalgia, rural, poignant |
| Clarinet | Warmth, storytelling, range | Versatile — lyric to demonic |
| Bassoon | Humor, solemnity, the earthy | Comic or darkly serious |
| Horn | The romantic, heroic, distant | Hunting, forests, nobility |
| Trumpet | Brilliance, announcement, martial | Fanfare, triumph, alarm |
| Trombone | Solemnity, doom, the sacred | Chorale, judgment, underworld |
| Tuba | Foundation, gravity, comic weight | Dark bass, lumbering, anchoring |
| Timpani | Fate, thunder, ceremony | Heartbeat of the orchestra |
| Harp | Heaven, transition, dreams | Glissandi = crossing thresholds |
| Celesta/glockenspiel | Magic, childhood, the miniature | Tinkling = otherworldly, delicate |
| Organ | The sacred, infinity, cosmic | Sustained = eternal; huge = divine |

## Register & Spacing

| Parameter | Quality | Reason |
|----------|---------|--------|
| High register | Ethereal, angelic, vulnerable | Upper overtones; thinner, lighter |
| Mid register | Human, warm, conversational | Vocal range; most natural |
| Low register | Dark, earthy, threatening | Bass = weight, shadow, foundation |
| Very high (extreme) | Tension, glass-like, alien | Beyond natural voice; eerie |
| Very low (extreme) | Rumble, dread, primal | Felt more than heard; subconscious |
| Wide spacing | Grandeur, openness, cosmos | Open voicing = space between = vastness |
| Close spacing | Intimacy, warmth, density | Voices together = closeness |
| Converging voices | Tension, collision | Approaching = impending impact |
| Diverging voices | Opening, release, expanding | Spreading apart = freedom |
