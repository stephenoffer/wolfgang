# Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart — Index

**Era:** Classical | **Nationality:** Austrian | **1756–1791**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 38–41, Piano Concertos 20–27, String Quartets K.387-465, The Marriage of Figaro, Don Giovanni, The Magic Flute, Requiem, Piano Sonatas K.331/457/576, Clarinet Concerto

**Style Summary:** The supreme melodist of the Classical era. Mozart's music is so perfectly balanced that the craftsmanship is invisible — it sounds effortless, natural, inevitable. But beneath that surface is meticulous voice-leading, harmonic subtlety (chromatic inflections that illuminate character), and formal mastery. His music is psychologically acute: a single unexpected chord can express more about a character's inner life than pages of text. Every note serves the melody; every harmony serves the emotion; every form serves the drama.
