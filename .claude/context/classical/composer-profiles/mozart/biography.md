# W.A. Mozart — Biography

The life of a man who heard music in every human situation — comedy, tragedy, seduction, prayer, rage, tenderness — and translated it all into sound before the age of 36. Mozart's genius was not merely precocity but absorption: every style he encountered on his extraordinary childhood travels was internalized, fused, and returned as something entirely his own. His music sounds effortless because the effort was metabolized completely.

## Life in Brief

| Year | Location | Role | Significance |
|------|----------|------|-------------|
| 1756 | Salzburg | Born | Son of Leopold Mozart, violinist, author of a famous violin treatise |
| 1762 | Munich, Vienna | Child performer | First tours; performs for Empress Maria Theresa aged 6 |
| 1763-66 | Paris, London, Netherlands | Grand Tour | 3.5 years; absorbs French, Italian, English styles; meets J.C. Bach in London — the singing allegro enters his DNA |
| 1769-73 | Italy (3 trips) | Opera student, prodigy | Studies with Padre Martini; writes Mitridate (age 14); absorbs Italian opera seria and buffa |
| 1773 | Vienna | Seeking position | Hears Haydn's Op. 20 quartets — contrapuntal ambition ignited |
| 1775-77 | Salzburg | Court musician | Increasingly frustrated under Archbishop Colloredo; violin concertos, early piano concertos |
| 1777-79 | Mannheim, Paris | Job-seeking tour | Falls in love with Aloysia Weber; mother dies in Paris; hears Mannheim orchestra's dynamic range |
| 1779-81 | Salzburg | Court organist | Idomeneo composed for Munich (opera seria masterpiece); trapped in provincial service |
| 1781 | Vienna | Breaks free | Dismissed by Colloredo ("with a kick on my arse"); begins freelance career |
| 1782-86 | Vienna | Peak years | Marries Constanze Weber; piano concertos K.449-491; Figaro; Haydn quartets; Freemason |
| 1787 | Vienna, Prague | Operatic triumph | Don Giovanni premieres in Prague; Leopold dies; begins to darken |
| 1788 | Vienna | Financial crisis | Three last symphonies (39, 40, 41) written in 6 weeks; few concerts; begging letters |
| 1789-90 | Vienna, Berlin, Frankfurt | Diminishing returns | Cosi fan tutte; tours fail to restore finances; health declining |
| 1791 | Vienna | Final year | Magic Flute, Clemenza di Tito, Clarinet Concerto, Requiem begun; dies December 5 |

## The Forces That Shaped the Music

### The Traveling Childhood

No composer before or since absorbed so many national styles so young. The tours were Leopold's commercial venture, but the musical result was a cosmopolitan synthesis no Salzburg-bound musician could have achieved.

| Source Encountered | What Mozart Absorbed | Where You Hear It |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| J.C. Bach (London, 1764) | Singing allegro, galant melody, keyboard concerto form | Piano concertos — the entire genre owes its shape to this meeting |
| Italian opera (1769-73) | Vocal line as supreme value; aria forms; dramatic pacing | Every instrumental melody — Mozart thinks vocally even in string quartets |
| Mannheim orchestra (1777) | Crescendo, dynamic contrasts, orchestral discipline | Symphonies 31 onward; Paris Symphony's opening crescendo |
| Haydn (Vienna, 1773+) | Sonata-form sophistication, motivic development, string quartet art | "Haydn" Quartets K.387-465 — dedicated "to my dear friend" |
| Bach & Handel (van Swieten, 1782) | Fugal technique, contrapuntal density | Jupiter finale, Requiem, late keyboard fugues |
| Freemasonry (1784) | Fraternity, enlightenment ideals, E-flat symbolism | Magic Flute, Masonic Funeral Music, key associations |

### Opera as the Key to Everything

Mozart's instrumental music IS opera without words. The piano concerto is a dialogue between soloist and orchestra — character against society. The string quartet is an ensemble scene — four distinct personalities negotiating. Even the solo piano sonata contains dramatic characters who interact, disagree, reconcile.

| Operatic Principle | Instrumental Application |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Aria = self-expression | Piano concerto solo passages; lyrical second themes |
| Recitative = conversation | Development sections; transitions between themes |
| Ensemble = multiple characters simultaneously | String quartet texture; concerto dialogue passages |
| Overture = sets emotional tone | Symphony first movements share operatic energy |
| Dramatic timing = silence, surprise, reversal | Unexpected rests, deceptive cadences, sudden minor shifts |

## Career Phases

| Phase | Period | Character | Defining Works |
|-------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Child Prodigy | 1762-73 | Astonishing imitation of every style encountered; charming but derivative | Early symphonies, K.16; Bastien und Bastienne |
| Salzburg Maturation | 1773-81 | Growing sophistication under Haydn's influence; frustration at provincial limits | Violin Concertos K.216-219, Sinfonia Concertante K.364, Idomeneo |
| Vienna Freelance Peak | 1781-86 | Supreme confidence; innovation in every genre; piano concertos as personal vehicle | Piano Concertos K.449-491, Figaro, "Haydn" Quartets, Symphonies 35-36 |
| Darkening Mastery | 1787-91 | Financial decline; deepening chromaticism; death-consciousness enters the music | Don Giovanni, last 3 Symphonies, Cosi, Magic Flute, Requiem, Clarinet Concerto |

### What Changes Between Phases

| Aspect | Salzburg (1773-81) | Vienna Peak (1781-86) | Final Period (1787-91) |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Melody | Elegant, balanced, Italianate | Psychologically nuanced, operatic depth | Stripped, stark, achingly simple |
| Harmony | Functional, clean modulations | Chromatic inflections, Neapolitan color | Dark chromaticism, remote modulations |
| Texture | Galant homophony, thin inner parts | Chamber transparency, wind independence | Contrapuntal density (Jupiter, Requiem) |
| Emotional range | Charm, grace, occasional storm | Full spectrum: comedy, tragedy, irony | Darkness and light interpenetrating |
| Orchestration | Strings-dominated, conventional winds | Winds as characters, clarinet emerges | Maximum economy — every note essential |
