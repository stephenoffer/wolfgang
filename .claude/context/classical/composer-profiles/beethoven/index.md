# Ludwig van Beethoven — Index

**Era:** Classical → Early Romantic | **Nationality:** German | **1770–1827**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 3–9, Piano Sonatas Op.13/27/57/106/111, String Quartets Op.18/59/130-135, Piano Concertos 3–5, Violin Concerto, Fidelio, Missa Solemnis

**Style Summary (Three Periods — DO NOT CONFLATE):**
- **Early (1792–1802):** Haydn/Mozart in temperament, but grander. Clear forms, balanced phrases, Classical clarity. Op. 18 quartets, Op. 13 sonata. Could be any talented Classical composer.
- **Middle (1803–1814):** The "heroic" period. Expanded scale, motivic development over long spans, greater harmonic ambition, drama as the organizing principle. Op. 59 quartets, Symphony 5, Waldstein, Appassionata. Beethoven as himself.
- **Late (1815–1827):** Transcendence of form itself. Fugue returns not as Baroque exercise but as expression of struggle. Extended variation as philosophical meditation. Radical key relationships. Inward, strange, sometimes ugly, always profound. Op. 131, Op. 111, Symphony 9.
