# Joseph Haydn — Index

**Era:** Classical | **Nationality:** Austrian | **1732–1809**

**Key Works:** Symphonies 88, 94 (Surprise), 100 (Military), 101 (Clock), 103, 104; String Quartets Op.33, Op.76; The Creation; The Seasons; Piano Sonata Hob.XVI/52; Trumpet Concerto

**Style Summary:** The father of the symphony and string quartet. Haydn invented the rules of Classical form by discovering that the most powerful musical experience comes from setting up expectations and then subverting them with wit, surprise, and structural ingenuity. His humor is not decoration — it IS the composition. A false recapitulation in the wrong key, a sudden silence where a climax was expected, a theme that returns disguised — these are Haydn's primary tools. His late London symphonies (93–104) represent Classical form at its most sophisticated and playful.
