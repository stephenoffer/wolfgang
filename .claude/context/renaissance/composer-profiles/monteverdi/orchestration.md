# Monteverdi — Scoring

Monteverdi is the first composer in this collection who *specifies* instruments,
and the specification is expressive rather than practical: in the Orfeo score
particular instruments belong to particular dramatic worlds.

---

## Voices

| Voice | Comfortable range | Role |
|-------|-------------------|------|
| Soprano | C4–G5 | Solo laments; the highest expressive register |
| Alto | G3–D5 | Inner parts; duets with soprano |
| Tenor | C3–G4 | His favourite solo voice — Orfeo himself |
| Bass | F2–D4 | Foundation; also underworld characters |

Solo writing sits within a tenth for declamation and opens out only at the
emotional peak. The duet of two equal high voices in parallel thirds and sixths,
suspending against each other, is a signature sound.

---

## Instruments and what they mean

| Instrument | Range (sounding) | Dramatic association |
|------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Violins (2) | G3–D6 | The pastoral world; ritornelli |
| Viole da braccio | G2–C5 | Consort body; the human world |
| Violone / contrabasso | E1–G3 | The bass foundation |
| Chitarrone / theorbo | Continuo | Intimate solo accompaniment |
| Harpsichord | Continuo | Recitative and narrative |
| Organo di legno | Continuo | Sacred, pastoral, gentle |
| Regal | Continuo | The underworld — deliberately harsh |
| Cornetti | A3–A5 | Brilliance, ceremony |
| Trombones (4) | E2–C5 | The underworld; solemnity |
| Recorders | F4–G6 | Pastoral, shepherds |
| Trumpets | C4–C6 | The opening toccata; royal ceremony |

The continuo choice is a dramatic act: the same recitative over a regal and over
an organo di legno is two different scenes.

---

## Textures

| Scoring | Effect | Where |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Solo voice + continuo | Words at maximum clarity | Recitative, lament |
| Two high voices + continuo | Intertwining suspensions, erotic or grieving | Chamber duets |
| Voice + obbligato instruments | Voice answered in its own terms | Vespers concertato |
| Full ensemble + continuo | Weight; ceremony | Choruses, tutti ritornelli |
| Instrumental ritornello | Structural punctuation | Between stanzas |

---

## What this scoring will not do

- Blend for its own sake. Instruments are chosen for what they mean, not for
  homogeneity of sound.
- Continuous full texture. The continuo is constant; everything above it comes
  and goes.
- Sustained writing at the extremes of a voice. The peaks are single moments.
